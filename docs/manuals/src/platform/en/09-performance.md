# 9. Performance

## What this is for

**Performance** shows how every spa is doing: revenue, bookings, website visits and how many visitors book. It uses totals only. You never see a client's name or record here.

## All spas

1. Click **Performance** in the sidebar.
2. Pick a range at the top right: **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days**, **This month** or **Last month**. The line under the title shows the exact dates.
3. Read the totals for all spas together:
   - **Revenue, net**: sales after refunds.
   - **Bookings**.
   - **Website visits**: visits to all spa websites.
   - **Online conversion**: the share of website visits that ended in a booking ("Visits that booked").
4. Read the table. Each row is one spa:
   - **Revenue**, **Bookings** (with how many were done, and how many were cancelled or no-show), **New clients**, **Visits** and **Conv.** (conversion). Click a heading to sort by it.
   - **Top sources**: where the bookings came from, for example "Online 55 · WhatsApp 35 · Walk-in 26".
   - **AI (month) →**: AI spend this month against the budget. Click the heading to open **AI usage** (chapter 16).
5. Click a spa to see its detail.

> **Tip:** Dates are business dates. A spa that stays open after midnight counts late sales on the right business day, using its own closing time.

> **Important:** The longest range is 92 days. Website visits are kept for 90 days only.

## One spa in detail

1. The top row of cards shows:
   - **Revenue, net**: sales minus refunds.
   - **Bookings**: with how many were completed, cancelled and no-show.
   - **New clients**.
   - **AI spend (month) →**: the AI spend against the budget. Click it for AI usage.
2. The second row shows how well the spa uses its time:
   - **Rebooking (30 days)**: how many completed visits were followed by a new booking.
   - **RevPATH**: revenue per available therapist hour (revenue without VAT ÷ therapist hours).
   - **Room utilisation**: hours booked against hours the rooms were open.
   - **Prepaid liability**: the value of gift cards, packages and memberships that clients have paid for but not used yet. "Matches ledger" means the figure agrees with the spa's accounts.
3. The charts show:
   - **Revenue**: per day, net of refunds.
   - **Bookings**: per day, excluding cancelled ones.
   - **Booking funnel**: **Website visits** → **Booking started** → **Booked online**.
   - **Website sources**: where website visitors came from (Instagram, Google, Direct, Google Business, QR code, WhatsApp) and how many of them booked.
   - **Booking channels**: non-cancelled bookings by source (Online, WhatsApp, Walk-in, Instagram, Staff / phone, AI agent and more), and online bookings by website source.

> **Tip:** A spa with many visits but few online bookings may need a clearer booking button on its website. A spa with no visits at all may not share its website link yet. Both are good reasons for a friendly call.
