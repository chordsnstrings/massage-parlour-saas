# About this manual

## What this is for

This manual explains how to run spamanagement.co from the owner's side. It covers the platform console, building spa websites, billing, and the day-to-day running of the server. Every task is written as short steps you can follow in order.

## Who this manual is for

- The **platform owner** (ARKS INVESTMENT), who runs spamanagement.co as a business.
- Every **super-admin**: a person on your team with full access to the platform console.

You do not need technical knowledge. Where a task needs a technical change (for example a new setting on the server), the manual says so and tells you to ask Claude to make it.

> **Important:** This manual is not for spa owners. Spa owners have their own manual, the Spa Owner's Manual, which covers the spa CRM.

## The three addresses

The platform has three main addresses. Each one has a different job.

| Address | What it is | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| **spamanagement.co** | The marketing website. New spas read about the product and apply here. | Visitors and new spas |
| **app.spamanagement.co** | The spa CRM. Spa owners and staff sign in here. | Spa owners, spa staff and super-admins |
| **admin.spamanagement.co** | The platform console. You manage every spa, invoice and website from here. You also open the Website Studio here. | Super-admins only |

Each spa also has its own public website. It lives at **{slug}.spamanagement.co** (for example saffron-spa.spamanagement.co) until the spa connects its own domain. Chapter 1 explains how these addresses fit together.

## How to read this manual

- Read **chapter 1** first. It is a one-page map of the whole platform.
- After that, go straight to the chapter you need. Each chapter stands on its own.
- Each chapter starts with **What this is for**: two or three lines that say what the chapter helps you do.
- Steps are numbered. Do them in order.
- Words in **bold** are the exact labels you see on the screen: buttons, menu items, field names and badges.
- A screenshot follows the step it shows. The numbered circles on a screenshot match the numbered list under it.
- Boxes that start with **Tip:** give a useful shortcut. Boxes that start with **Important:** warn you about something that is easy to get wrong or cannot be undone.
- The **Glossary** at the end explains the words used in this manual.

### About the screenshots

- All screenshots come from a **demo copy** of the platform with made-up spas, people and numbers. No real customer data is shown.
- Some web addresses in the screenshots end in **localhost** because the demo copy runs on a test computer. On the live platform they read **spamanagement.co**.
- The console screens are in English. They look the same on the live platform.

## Getting help

- For questions about the platform or this manual, write to **ask@spamanagement.co**. It is the only spamanagement.co email address used anywhere on the platform.
- For a change to how the platform works, ask Claude. Chapter 19 explains how a change reaches the live platform.

> **Important:** Never paste a password, an API key or any other secret into a chat, an email or a document. Chapter 24 explains where secrets belong.
