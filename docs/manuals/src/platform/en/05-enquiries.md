# 5. Enquiries from the Contact page

## What this is for

People who are not ready to apply can write to you from the Contact page on spamanagement.co. This chapter shows how to answer them and keep track of who you have already called.

## Where enquiries come from

1. A visitor opens **Contact** on spamanagement.co.
2. Under **Send us a message**, they fill **Your name**, **Phone**, **Email**, **Spa name** and **What do you need?** (up to 2,000 characters). Every field is required.
3. They click **Send message**. The page thanks them: "Thanks — we'll reply within one working day."

   ![The Contact page form.](../../../shots/platform/enq-contact-form.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The enquiry form **Send us a message**.
   2. The contact email shown to visitors (**ask@spamanagement.co**).

4. You get an email with the enquiry. When you reply to that email, your reply goes straight to the visitor.
5. The **Enquiries** badge in the console counts the enquiries that are still **New**.

> **Tip:** The email address, WhatsApp number, phone number and office address on the Contact page come from **Company** settings (chapter 15). If no company email is set, the page shows ask@spamanagement.co.

> **Important:** To stop spam, one network can send at most 5 enquiries per hour and 20 per day. The form also has a hidden trap that catches most automatic spam.

> **Tip:** Messages and booking requests from a spa's own website never come here. They go to that spa's dashboard (**Inbox & follow-ups** → **Enquiries**). This includes the booking form of an HTML design connected to the CRM (chapter 12, "Designs connected to the CRM").

## Answer and close an enquiry

1. Click **Enquiries** in the sidebar.
2. Use the filters **New**, **Contacted**, **Closed** or **All**. Each filter shows how many enquiries it holds.
3. To find someone, type in **Search name, email or spa**. You can also search by phone number.
4. The columns are **From** (name and spa), **Contact** (email and phone), **Message**, **Sent** and **Status**.

   ![Enquiries.](../../../shots/platform/enq-list.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The status filters.
   2. The search box.
   3. The status badge (**New**).

5. Click a name to open the enquiry.
6. Read **What they need**.
7. Under **Contact**, choose how to reply:
   - **Call** starts a phone call (on a phone or a computer that can make calls).
   - **WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with a ready greeting to that number. Check the message, finish it and tap Send yourself.
   - **Email** opens your email program with the subject "Your enquiry to spamanagement.co".
8. Under **Follow-up**, choose the new **Status**:
   - **New**: "Not answered yet (counted on the nav badge)."
   - **Contacted**: "You replied; waiting on them."
   - **Closed**: "Done — signed up, not a fit or spam."
9. Optional: write an **Internal note**, for example what you agreed on the phone. Only super-admins see it.
10. Click **Save**. A message confirms it, for example "Marked contacted" or "Note saved".

> **Tip:** On a phone, the same buttons are at the top of the **Contact** card. Tap **Call** to ring the person straight away.

> **Tip:** **Last handled** on the enquiry shows who changed it last and when. Every change is also written to the **Audit log**.
