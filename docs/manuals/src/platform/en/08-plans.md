# 8. Plans & prices

## What this is for

This page holds the plans that new spas can choose on the pricing page and the application form. Changing a plan here never changes what existing spas pay. Their price only changes when you change their own subscription (chapter 6).

## Read the plan cards

1. Click **Plans & prices** in the sidebar. The page says: "Prices apply to new spas. Existing spas keep their agreed price until you change their subscription."
2. Each card shows:
   - The plan's name and a badge: **Active** (offered to new spas), **Hidden** (not offered) or **Legacy** (the old yearly plan).
   - The price per month (or per year for a yearly plan), the price per 12 months, and "excl. VAT".
   - The features, for example "AI & Instagram automation · Marketing tools · More branches", or "Core only · one branch" for Standard.
   - The description.
   - **Setup fee**, **Trial** and **Spas on it** (how many spas use the plan).

Your plans are:

| Plan | Price (excl. VAT) | Setup fee | Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Premium** | AED 3,000 per month (AED 36,000 per 12 months) | AED 14,000 | Everything: AI & Instagram automation, marketing tools, more branches |
| **Standard** | AED 2,000 per month (AED 24,000 per 12 months) | AED 9,000 | Core CRM and website, one branch |
| **Yearly (legacy)** | AED 24,000 per year | none | Every feature. Kept for existing spas until their renewal; not offered to new spas |

## Create or edit a plan

1. Click **New plan**, or **Edit** on a plan's card.
2. Fill the fields:
   - **Name**: what spas see, for example "Premium".
   - **Code**: a short internal name. The code of a built-in plan cannot be changed ("Used by the app (pricing page, sign-up links): fixed.").
   - **Price per 12 months (AED)**: for a monthly plan, 12 × the monthly fee (AED 3,000 per month = 36,000). Excl. VAT.
   - **Default payment plan**: **12 monthly invoices** or **One-time annual**. New spas on this plan get this payment plan.
   - **Setup fee (AED)**.
   - **Trial days**.
   - **Description**: shown on the plan.
   - **Features**: **Premium features** (AI & Instagram automation, marketing tools, more branches) or **Standard features** (core only, one branch).
   - **Sort order**: the order in which plans are listed. Lower numbers come first.
3. Tick **Available for new spas** to offer the plan on the pricing page and the application form. Untick it to hide the plan.
4. Click **Save**. A message confirms "Plan saved".

> **Important:** Changing a plan's price only affects spas that join from now on. To change what an existing spa pays, edit that spa's **Subscription**, or give it a discount (chapter 6).

> **Tip:** If you see "That code is already used.", choose another **Code**. Each plan needs its own code.

## Delete, archive and restore

1. Click **Delete** on a plan's card. A sheet opens and says exactly what will happen.
2. A custom plan that no spa and no application uses is deleted for good. The sheet says "This can't be undone." Click **Delete plan**.
3. A plan that spas or applications use, and every built-in plan (Premium, Standard, Yearly (legacy)), is archived instead. Click **Archive plan**.

   ![A plan in use is archived, not deleted.](../../../shots/platform/plans-archive-sheet.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. What will happen: how many spas and applications use the plan, and what archiving does.
   2. **Archive plan**.

4. A message confirms it, for example "Standard archived. Restore it from Archived plans."
5. An archived plan disappears from this page, from the pricing page and from new sign-ups. Spas already on it keep their plan and price until you change their subscription.

**Bring an archived plan back:**

1. At the bottom of the page, click **Archived plans (1)** (the number shows how many there are).
2. Click **Restore** next to the plan. A message confirms "… restored. Edit it to offer it to new spas again."
3. Click **Edit** on the restored plan, tick **Available for new spas** and click **Save** to offer it again.

> **Important:** If a pending applicant chose a plan that you archive, you choose another plan for them when you accept the application (chapter 4).

> **Tip:** To stop offering a plan for a while without archiving it, untick **Available for new spas**. The card then shows **Hidden**.
