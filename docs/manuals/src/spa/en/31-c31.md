# 31. Troubleshooting and questions

## What this is for

Quick answers to the questions spa owners ask most. If you're stuck, email **ask@spamanagement.co**.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can clients pay by card online?

No. The CRM records payments only. Clients pay at the spa: in cash, on your own card terminal or by bank transfer. You record the payment at the till (chapter 14).

### Why didn't the client get a WhatsApp message?

Nothing is sent automatically. Open **Inbox & follow-ups** → **WhatsApp**, press **Open in WhatsApp** on the message, send it in WhatsApp, then press **Mark sent** (chapter 16).

### How do I change my website's design or photos?

On **Website studio** → **Website**, use **Request a change** (chapter 24). Upload new photos to **Media** first and mention them in your request. Our studio makes and publishes the change. Under **Your requests**, the request then shows **Done**, with the studio's reply. You can also write to **ask@spamanagement.co**. You change services, prices, treatment photos and therapist photos yourself (chapters 7 and 8).

### My website isn't live yet. What can I do?

Our studio builds and publishes it for you. On **Website studio** → **Website**, the **Your website** card shows "Our studio is crafting your website" before the studio starts, then **In progress**, then **Live** (chapter 24). Meanwhile, complete your spa details, services and prices, opening hours and contact details (chapter 6): the studio writes your texts from them. Upload your photos to **Media** and tell the studio what you would like with **Request a change**.

### A menu item is missing.

Your role may not include that page (chapter 9), or it is a Premium feature (chapter 1). Premium pages are left out of the menu on the Standard plan.

### I see "Price on request".

The service has no price, or its prices are hidden on the website. Check **Show prices on the website** under **Settings** → **Spa profile**, and the service's **Price on website** (chapter 7).

### A therapist is missing from the calendar or from online booking.

Check that they are **Bookable** and **Active**, that the service is ticked under **Skills**, that they have shifts that day, and that they are not on leave (chapters 8 and 18).

### A therapist is missing from my website.

Open **Team & roles** → **Staff** and edit the person. Check that **Show on website** and **Active** are on. Websites with an HTML design also need a photo (chapter 8).

### A treatment is missing from my website, or shows an old photo or text.

Your website shows only services that are **Active** and **Bookable online** and have at least one duration. Edit the service to change its photo or description (chapters 7 and 24). If a service has no photo or description of its own and the website's design has a treatment with the same name, your website shows the design's picture and text. Add your own in the service. On an HTML design, two services with the same English name show as one treatment, with all their durations and the lowest price. Give them different names to show both.

### My website shows the wrong area, phone or opening hours.

Correct them in **Settings** → **Spa profile** (main branch), **Settings** → **Branches** or **Settings** → **Opening hours** (chapters 6 and 28). Your website changes on its next page load.

### A booking from my website is not on the calendar.

It is a booking request in **Inbox & follow-ups** → **Enquiries**. Check the time, add the booking to the calendar and confirm on WhatsApp (chapter 17).

### The booking won't save: "… is not free right now" or "That time clashes with another booking".

The therapist, room or equipment is already reserved at that time. The CRM blocks double bookings. Pick another time or another therapist, or choose **Any (automatic)** so the CRM picks a free one (chapter 10).

### I can't void a sale.

**Void sale** is only for mistakes on a day that is still open. Sales with packages, memberships or gift cards can't be voided. Record a **Refund** instead (chapter 14).

### I can't change an old expense.

The period is closed: "That date is in a closed period. Pick a date after the lock date." Post the correction in the open period (chapter 22).

### The dashboard is read-only.

An invoice is unpaid past the grace period. Pay it on **Billing** (chapter 29). Your website and online booking keep working. Full access returns as soon as the payment is received. If our team paused your account, the bar says "Your account is paused for late payment…". Then full access returns when our team resumes your account after your payment (chapter 29).

### Client phone numbers show as hidden.

Only the **Owner**, **Manager** and **Receptionist** roles see client phone numbers. Everyone else sees them masked (chapter 9).

### My two-step verification code is not accepted.

Check that the time on your phone is set automatically. Or press **Use a backup code** and type one of your backup codes (chapters 2 and 5). Lost both your phone and your backup codes? Email **ask@spamanagement.co** for help.

### The app doesn't notify me.

Turn on **Notifications on this device** in **Account & security**, on each phone or computer separately. On an iPhone, install the app first and open it from the Home Screen (chapters 4 and 5).

### The AI has stopped working.

Your monthly AI allowance may be used up: "AI paused: monthly AI budget reached — contact us. AI restarts on the 1st." Email **ask@spamanagement.co** to raise it (chapter 26).

### Who do I contact?

Email **ask@spamanagement.co**, or press **Message us on WhatsApp** on **Billing**.

## If a page isn't found

If you open an address that doesn't exist, for example from an old bookmark, you see **Page not found**.

1. Press **Back to the dashboard home**.
2. Find the page again in the left menu.
