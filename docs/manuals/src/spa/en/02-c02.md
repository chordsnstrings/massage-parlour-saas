# 2. Getting your account and signing in

## What this is for

This chapter shows how a new spa applies, how you sign in safely every day, and what to do if you forget your password. Your team members join by invitation (chapter 9).

## Apply for your spa

1. Go to **app.spamanagement.co**. Under "New to spamanagement?", press **Apply for your spa**.
2. Under **Your login**, fill in **Your name**, **Work email** and **Password**. The password needs at least 10 characters.
3. Under **Your spa**, fill in your **Mobile number**. Use a UAE mobile, for example 050 123 4567.
4. Fill in the **Spa name**.
5. Choose a **Web address**. Wait until you see "*address* is available" in green.
6. Choose your **Emirate** and type your **Street address**.
7. If you like, add your logo under **Logo (optional)**.
8. Choose a **Plan** and a **Preferred start date**. Add anything we should know under **Notes (optional)**.
9. Press **Send application**.

   ![The application form for a new spa.](../../../shots/spa/c02-s01-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Your login**: your name, work email and password.
   2. **Mobile number**.
   3. **Web address**, with the green "is available" check.
   4. **Emirate** and **Street address**.
   5. **Plan**, showing the setup fee and the monthly price.
   6. **Send application**.

> **Tip:** The web address can use 3 to 40 lowercase letters, numbers or hyphens. If you see "That address is taken." or "This name is reserved.", try another one.

> **Tip:** The logo can be a PNG, JPG or WebP file of up to 1 MB. You can add or change it later in **Settings**.

> **Important:** A password that has appeared in a known data leak is refused. Choose a new password that you do not use anywhere else.

If you see "We couldn't confirm you're not a robot. Please try again.", wait a moment and press **Send application** again.

## Waiting for approval

1. After you send the form, you see **Application received** with the status **Waiting for approval**.
2. You do not need to do anything. We email you as soon as your application is reviewed.
3. When it is approved, this page shows **Your spa is ready**. Press **Open your dashboard**.
4. The first time, you are asked to set up two-step verification before the dashboard opens (chapter 5). Your spa requires it for owners and managers.

   ![Your application page while we review it.](../../../shots/spa/c02-s02-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Status**: **Waiting for approval**.
   2. **What you sent**: the details from your form.
   3. **Sign out**.

If the application is not approved, the page shows **Application not approved** with the reason. Write to ask@spamanagement.co if you have questions.

## Sign in

1. Open **app.spamanagement.co**, or open the installed app (chapter 4).
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Press **Sign in**.
4. If two-step verification is on, type the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (next section).
5. If your login belongs to more than one spa, choose the spa on **Choose a spa** (see "Choose a spa, switch spa, apply for another spa" below).

   ![The sign-in page.](../../../shots/spa/c02-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Email**.
   2. **Password**.
   3. **Forgot password?**
   4. **Sign in**.

What the error messages mean:

- "Wrong email or password.": check both and try again.
- "Too many failed attempts. Your account is locked for a while — try again later.": wait, then try again. Or reset your password.
- "Please verify your email first.": open the email we sent you and press the link in it.

To sign out, open the profile menu (your name at the top of the left menu) and press **Sign out**.

## Two-step verification at sign-in

Two-step verification protects your account with a code from your phone. Chapter 5 shows how to switch it on.

1. After your password, the **Two-step verification** page asks for a code.
2. Open your authenticator app and type the code in **6-digit code**.
3. On your own phone or computer only, tick **Trust this device for 30 days**. You then skip the code on this device for 30 days.
4. Press **Verify**.
5. Lost your phone? Press **Use a backup code** and type one of the backup codes you saved.

   ![Enter the code from your authenticator app.](../../../shots/spa/c02-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **6-digit code**.
   2. **Trust this device for 30 days**.
   3. **Verify**.
   4. **Use a backup code**.

> **Important:** Each backup code works only once. Never tick **Trust this device for 30 days** on a shared computer, such as the reception computer.

If you see "That code is not correct." or "That code has expired.", wait for the next code in the app and type it.

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, press **Forgot password?**
2. Type your **Email** and press **Send reset link**.
3. You see "If that email has an account, a reset link is on its way."
4. Open the email and press the link in it.
5. Type a **New password** of at least 10 characters.
6. Press **Set password**. You see "Password updated. Please sign in."
7. Sign in with your new password.

   ![Ask for a reset link by email.](../../../shots/spa/c02-s05-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Email**.
   2. **Send reset link**.
   3. The message that the link is on its way.

   ![Choose your new password.](../../../shots/spa/c02-s05-b.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **New password**.
   2. **Set password**.

> **Tip:** If the link says "This link is invalid or has expired.", press **Request a new one** and use the newest email.

> **Important:** Changing your password signs you out on your other devices. Sign in again there with the new password.

## Choose a spa, switch spa, apply for another spa

1. If your login belongs to several spas, **Choose a spa** lists them with your role in each one.
2. Click a spa to open its dashboard.
3. Inside a spa, open the profile menu (your name at the top of the left menu) and press **Switch spa** to come back to this list.
4. To add another business under the same login, press **Apply for another spa**. The form is the same as in "Apply for your spa".

   ![Pick the spa you want to work in.](../../../shots/spa/c02-s06-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. A spa card with your role (here **Owner**).
   2. **Apply for another spa**.
   3. The account menu (your initials). It has **Account & security** and **Sign out**.

> **Tip:** If your login belongs to only one spa, you skip this page and go straight to your dashboard.

The profile menu inside a spa has **Account & security**, **Switch spa**, **Install app** (when your browser offers it) and **Sign out**.

## How your staff join (invitation)

1. You send an invitation from **Team & roles** → **Team** (chapter 9).
2. The person opens the link in the email. They see **You're invited** and "Join *spa* as *role*."
3. A new person types **Your name** and a **Password**, then presses **Create account & join**.
4. A person who already has a login presses **Sign in to accept**, signs in, then presses **Accept invitation**.

   ![What an invited team member sees.](../../../shots/spa/c02-s07-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The spa they are joining, and the role (here **Therapist**).
   2. **Your name**.
   3. **Password**.
   4. **Create account & join**.

> **Important:** An invited owner or manager is asked to set up two-step verification the first time, before the dashboard opens (chapter 5).

> **Important:** An invitation link works for 7 days. After that the person sees **Invitation expired**. Send a new invitation from **Team**.

If the person is signed in with a different email, they see "This invitation is for *email*, but you're signed in as *other email*." They must sign out and use the email the invitation was sent to.
