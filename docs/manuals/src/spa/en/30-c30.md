# 30. Import, export and audit log

## What this is for

Bring your clients, your menu and your products in from a spreadsheet. Take your data out to Excel whenever you like. And see who changed what in your spa.

## Import clients, menu or products

You can import from Excel (.xlsx) or CSV. Excel, Google Sheets, Fresha and most booking systems can export one of these. You check every column before anything is saved.

1. Open **Settings** → **Import & export**.
2. Choose a card: **Clients**, **Service menu** or **Products**. Each card says which columns it reads.
3. Want a ready layout? Press **Template** on the card to download a template file with the right columns.
4. Press **Import clients** (or **Import service menu**, or **Import products**).

   ![Import & export.](../../../shots/spa/c30-s01-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. An import card (**Clients**), with **Template** and **Import clients**.
   2. **Export to Excel**.
   3. **Full export**.
   4. **Signed intake forms**, inside the same card as **Export to Excel**.
   5. **Recent imports**.

5. **Upload**: drop your Excel or CSV file onto the page, or press **browse**. Up to 5 MB. The CRM reads it and shows how many rows it found, how many have errors and how many already exist.
6. **Check**: under **Match your columns**, check which column of your file goes into which field. The CRM guesses from the headers. Change anything that looks wrong; choose **Don't import** for columns you don't want.
7. Choose what to do with records you already have, for example **Clients you already have (same mobile number)**: **Update their details** or **Leave them as they are**.
8. Look at the **Preview**: the first rows as they will be saved, each with a status such as **New**, **Update**, **Skip** or **Error**.
9. Press **Import *n* clients** (or menu items, or products).

   ![Check the columns before importing.](../../../shots/spa/c30-s01-b.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The steps **Upload**, **Check** and **Done**.
   2. **Match your columns**.
   3. **Clients you already have**: **Update their details** or **Leave them as they are**.
   4. **Preview**, each row marked **New**.
   5. **Import 25 clients**.

10. **Done** shows how many rows were **Created**, **Updated**, **Skipped** and had **Errors**. If some rows need fixing, press **Download error file**. Fix those rows in your spreadsheet and import that file.

What each import does:

- **Clients**: name, mobile, gender, birthday, tags, notes and language. Existing clients are matched by mobile number. Email addresses are recognised but not imported, because client messages go by WhatsApp.
- **Service menu**: one row per service and duration, with the category, the English and Arabic names and the price. Rows with the same service name become one service with several durations.
- **Products**: name, SKU, unit, cost, price and stock on hand. The opening stock is valued at cost in your accounts.

**Recent imports** lists your last ten imports, with the result of each and, where needed, the error file.

> **Tip:** An old .xls file must first be saved as .xlsx in Excel. A file larger than 5 MB must be split into smaller files.

## Export to Excel and full export

### Export to Excel

1. In **Export to Excel**, choose **What to export**: **Clients**, **Bookings**, **Sales**, **Sale lines**, **Payments**, **Expenses** or **Products & stock**.
2. Choose **From** and **To** if you like. Leave the dates empty for everything.
3. Press **Download Excel**. The file opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

The dates filter clients by when they were added; bookings, sales and payments by business day; and expenses by expense date. Products export today's stock.

### Full export

1. In **Full export**, press **Download .xlsx**.
2. You get everything in your account in one Excel workbook: a README sheet, then one sheet per table. It includes clients, bookings, sales, payments, packages, gift cards, the ledger, payroll, stock, website pages and settings.

> **Important:** Passwords, access tokens and uploaded files are never included in the full export. Only the Owner can download it, and each download is recorded in the activity log.

### Signed intake forms

Press **Download PDFs (.zip)** to get every signed intake form and waiver as a PDF (signature, answers and record hash) in one .zip file.

> **Tip:** For roles without access to client phone numbers, phone numbers are left out of the export.

## Audit log

1. Open **Settings** → **Audit log**. You can also press **View full audit log** on the **Spa profile** tab.
2. Filter by **Person**, **Action**, **From** and **To**, then press **Apply**. Press **Reset** to clear the filters.
3. Each row shows **When**, **Person**, **Action**, **Record** and **IP address**, newest first.

   ![The audit log.](../../../shots/spa/c30-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The filters.
   2. An entry: the person and what they did (here a refund).
   3. The **IP address**.

The audit log is read-only.

> **Tip:** **System** means the CRM did it by itself, for example an automation. **Former user** is someone whose login was removed. When our support team helps you in your dashboard, you see "*name* · platform support". Every change they make is recorded.
