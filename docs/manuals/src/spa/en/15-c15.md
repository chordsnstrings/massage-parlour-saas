# 15. Packages, memberships, gift cards and promo codes

## What this is for

Prepaid offers bring clients back: session packages, monthly memberships, gift cards with printable vouchers, and promo codes. You create them here and sell them from **Sales** (chapter 14).

Everything is on **Services & menu** → **Packages & gifts**. The page has four tabs: **Packages**, **Memberships**, **Gift cards** and **Promo codes**.

> **Important:** Money paid in advance is held as a liability in your accounts until it is used. A package session, a membership period or a gift card balance becomes revenue only when the client uses it.

## Packages

A package lets a client prepay for several sessions. Each visit uses one up.

1. Open **Services & menu** → **Packages & gifts**, tab **Packages**, and press **New package**.
2. Type the **Name**, for example 10 × Thai 60, and the **Name (Arabic)**.
3. Under **Treatments included**, choose each treatment and type its number of sessions.
4. Type the **Package price (AED, incl. VAT)**.
5. Type **Valid for (days)**: how long the client has to use it (at least 7 days).
6. Add a **Description** if you like.
7. Leave **Available for sale** ticked and press **Create package**.

   ![Create a package.](../../../shots/spa/c15-s01-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Name**.
   2. **Package price (AED, incl. VAT)**.
   3. **Valid for (days)**.
   4. **Treatments included**.
   5. **Create package**.

Each package card shows how long it is valid and how many were sold. Press **Pause** to stop selling it, and **Activate** to sell it again.

> **Important:** **Edit** changes apply to new sales only. Packages already sold keep their terms.

> **Tip:** Packages that pass their end date are closed automatically every night.

## Membership plans

A membership is a monthly plan with included sessions and a member discount. It keeps regulars coming back.

1. Open the **Memberships** tab and press **New plan**.
2. Type the **Name**, for example Monthly wellness.
3. Type the **Monthly fee (AED, incl. VAT)** and the **Member discount on extras (%)**.
4. If the plan includes treatments, choose them under **Included sessions each month (optional)**.
5. Leave **Open for new members** ticked and press **Create plan**.

   ![Membership plans.](../../../shots/spa/c15-s02-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. A plan with its monthly fee and benefits (here 1 Swedish massage and 10% off extras, AED 450 a month).
   2. **Active members**.
   3. **New plan**.

6. Sell or renew a membership at the till with **Sell / renew a membership…** (chapter 14). At checkout, tick **Use an included session from membership "*name*" · *n* left** on the member's service line. The member discount is ticked for you on other service lines, and can be unticked.

> **Tip:** A week before a membership period ends, a renewal reminder is prepared in the WhatsApp queue (chapter 16).

## Gift cards and printable vouchers

1. Sell a gift card from **Sales** with **Gift card** (chapter 14). It gets a code like ABCD-EFGH.
2. Open the **Gift cards** tab. It lists the latest 200 gift cards with their **Code**, who they are **For**, the **Issued** and **Expires** dates, the **Value**, the **Balance** and the **Status**.

   ![Gift cards sold.](../../../shots/spa/c15-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Code**.
   2. **Balance**: what is left to spend.
   3. **Status**: **Active**, **Redeemed**, **Expired** or **Void**.
   4. **Voucher**.

3. Press **Voucher** on a gift card. The printable voucher opens.
4. Choose the **Paper size**: **A6** or **A5**.
5. Press **Print voucher**, or **Share on WhatsApp** to send it.
6. Press **Edit voucher** to change what the voucher shows: **For (recipient name)**, **Recipient mobile**, **Message on the voucher** and **Show** (the amount, or a treatment). Press **Save voucher**.

   ![A printable gift voucher.](../../../shots/spa/c15-s03-b.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The voucher, with the amount, the recipient, the message, the code and the QR code.
   2. **Paper size**.
   3. **Print voucher**.
   4. **Share on WhatsApp**.
   5. **Edit voucher**.

The QR code opens a page on your website that shows only the balance and the expiry date. Press **Open the check page** to see it yourself.

> **Tip:** To use a gift card, choose **Gift card** as the payment at checkout and type the code or scan the QR (chapter 14).

> **Tip:** A treatment voucher shows the treatment instead of the amount. At checkout it still pays up to its balance. The value, balance and expiry always come from the sale.

If the voucher has no mobile number, WhatsApp asks which chat to send it to.

## Promo codes

Create codes for Instagram offers, Ramadan or corporate partners.

1. Open the **Promo codes** tab and press **New code**.
2. Type the **Code**: 3 to 20 letters, numbers or dashes, for example NATIONALDAY15.
3. Choose the **Type**: **Percent off** or **AED off**. Type the **Value**.
4. If you like, set **Starts**, **Ends** and **Maximum uses**. Leave **Maximum uses** empty for unlimited.
5. Press **Create code**.

   ![Create a promo code.](../../../shots/spa/c15-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Code**.
   2. **Type** and **Value**.
   3. **Starts**.
   4. **Ends**.
   5. **Maximum uses**.
   6. **Create code**.

The list shows each code with its **Discount**, the dates it is **Valid**, how often it was **Used** and its **Status**.

You can add a promo code to a campaign message, so clients receive it on WhatsApp (chapter 25).

> **Important:** The till does not apply a promo code by itself. When a client shows a code, give the discount at checkout in the **Discount** field of the item or in **Discount (AED)** (chapter 14).

If you see "That code already exists.", choose another code. A percent discount can be at most 100%.
