# Glossary

## What this is for

Short explanations of the words used in this manual, in alphabetical order.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AED** | UAE dirham. Every amount in the CRM is in AED. |
| **Area shown on the website** | The branch setting, with an optional Arabic version, for the area name your website shows, for example "Dubai Marina, Dubai". Empty means the city from the address. Only the main branch's area is used (chapters 6 and 28). |
| **Ask AI** | The AI helper in the top bar (Premium). You ask a question about your spa in plain words and it answers from your own data. |
| **Audit log** | The record of every important change in your spa: who did what, and when (chapter 30). |
| **Authenticator app** | A phone app, such as Google Authenticator, 1Password or Authy, that shows the 6-digit code for two-step verification. |
| **Auto-confirm** | An online booking from a returning client that is confirmed at once, without waiting for you (chapter 6). |
| **Backup code** | A one-time code you can use instead of the authenticator app if you lose your phone. Each one works once. |
| **Blocklist** | A flag on a client the team should not book. The team sees a warning when this client books (chapter 13). |
| **Booking reference** | The short code of a booking, for example YJVAC. You can search for it. |
| **Booking request** | What a guest sends from the booking form of a website design that has its own form. It lands in **Enquiries** with the treatment, length, date, time, guests and therapist. It is not a booking: you confirm it on WhatsApp and add it to the calendar (chapter 17). |
| **Booking widget** | A button you add to another website. It opens your booking page in a pop-up (chapter 12). |
| **Branch** | One location of your spa, with its own calendar, rooms, till and business day. More than one branch needs Premium (chapter 28). |
| **Buffer** | Free minutes before or after a treatment to prepare the room or change the linen (chapter 7). |
| **Business day** | The day a sale or booking belongs to. It ends at the time set in **Business day ends at**, not at midnight, so late-night sales count on the right day. |
| **Campaign** | A WhatsApp message written for a group of clients, for example a win-back or birthday offer (Premium, chapter 25). |
| **Click-to-send** | The way the CRM sends WhatsApp messages: it writes the message and opens WhatsApp, and a person presses send. |
| **Commission** | The amount in AED a therapist earns for a completed booking. It is entered on the booking (chapter 11) and paid through payroll. |
| **Daily close (Z-report)** | The end-of-day count of the cash drawer, compared with what the till expects. Closing locks the day (chapter 14). |
| **Duplicate** | Two client profiles that look like the same person, for example with the same mobile. You can merge them (chapter 13). |
| **Equipment** | Kits or machines a treatment needs, such as hot-stone kits. Each booking reserves one free unit (chapter 7). |
| **Gift card** | A prepaid amount with a code like ABCD-EFGH. It is used as a payment method at checkout (chapter 15). |
| **HTML design** | A finished website design file our studio uploads for your site, shown exactly as designed, for example one made just for your spa. When our studio connects it to your dashboard, it takes your menu, team and contact details live from there (chapter 24). |
| **Intake form** | The health questions and waiver a client signs on the tablet before their first treatment. The signed form is stored as a PDF (chapter 13). |
| **Liability** | Money a client has paid in advance (packages, memberships, gift cards) that is not yet used. It becomes revenue when it is used. |
| **Live (website)** | Your website is published and clients can see it. On the **Website** page the pill says **Live**; before that it says **In progress** (chapter 24). |
| **Membership** | A monthly plan with included sessions and a member discount (chapter 15). |
| **No-show** | A client who did not come to a booking. |
| **Occupancy** | How full your therapists are: booked therapist hours compared with shift hours. |
| **Owner** | The role with full access to your spa, including the subscription and roles. |
| **Package** | A prepaid set of sessions, for example 5 × Deep tissue 60. Each visit uses one session (chapter 15). |
| **Pending** | A booking that is not confirmed or finished yet. Online bookings start as pending until you confirm them. |
| **Permission** | One thing a role is allowed to do, for example **Take payments** or **View accounts** (chapter 9). |
| **Plan** | Your subscription: **Standard** or **Premium** (chapter 1). |
| **Premium** | The plan with AI & Instagram automation, marketing tools and more than one branch. AED 14,000 setup + AED 3,000 a month, excluding VAT. |
| **Promo code** | A discount code for an offer, for example RAMADAN20 (chapter 15). |
| **PWA (installed app)** | The CRM installed on your phone or computer like an app, named after your spa (chapter 4). |
| **QR code** | A square code that a phone camera can scan. It is used on gift vouchers and booking posters. |
| **Read-only** | A state your dashboard enters when a platform invoice is unpaid past the grace period. You can look, but not change anything; your website and online booking keep working (chapter 29). |
| **Refund** | Money given back to a client after a sale. You give the money back first, then record it (chapter 14). |
| **Request a change** | The form on the **Website** page where you ask our studio for any change to your website other than what comes from your dashboard (menu, prices, team, contact details, hours). The studio replies under **Your requests** (chapter 24). |
| **Role** | The set of permissions a team member has: Owner, Manager, Receptionist, Therapist, Accountant, Content editor or a custom role (chapter 9). |
| **Shift** | The hours a therapist works on a day. Clients can only book a therapist during a shift (chapter 8). |
| **Show on website** | The switch in a therapist's form. Only people with it on appear on your website; on an HTML design they also need a photo. It starts off for new staff (chapter 8). |
| **Skills** | The services a therapist performs. Only these can be booked with them (chapter 8). |
| **Slug (web address)** | The short name of your spa in its addresses, for example "serenity" in serenity.spamanagement.co. |
| **Standard** | The plan with every core feature and one branch. AED 9,000 setup + AED 2,000 a month, excluding VAT. |
| **Tax invoice** | A receipt with VAT. A simplified tax invoice is printed for every sale; a full tax invoice adds the customer's name, address and TRN (chapter 14). |
| **Template (WhatsApp)** | The standard text of a kind of WhatsApp message, in English and Arabic, with variables such as {first_name} (chapter 16). |
| **Tip** | Money a client gives on top of the bill. It is kept for the therapist. |
| **TRN** | Tax Registration Number: the 15-digit VAT number of a VAT-registered business in the UAE. |
| **Turn list** | Today's order of therapists for walk-ins, so walk-ins are shared fairly (chapter 10). |
| **Two-step verification (2FA)** | Signing in with your password plus a 6-digit code from your phone (chapter 5). |
| **VAT** | Value added tax, 5% in the UAE. Prices in the CRM include VAT. |
| **Void** | Cancelling a sale entered by mistake on a day that is still open. The sale drops out of the day's totals (chapter 14). |
| **Waitlist** | Clients waiting for a treatment on a fully booked day. When a slot frees up, a WhatsApp message is prepared (chapter 11). |
| **Walk-in** | A client who comes in without a booking (chapter 10). |
| **Website studio** | The menu item with your **Website** page and your **Media** library. Our studio (the spamanagement.co team) designs, writes and publishes your website; you keep its menu, team and contact details up to date (chapter 24). |
| **Win-back target** | A client who has not visited for 60 days or more. |
| **WPS** | Wage Protection System: the UAE system for paying staff salaries through the bank (chapter 20). |
