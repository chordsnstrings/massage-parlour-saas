# 8. Staff and shifts

## What this is for

Add the therapists who perform your treatments: what each one can do, how they are paid and when they work. A therapist does not need a login to appear on the calendar.

## Add a therapist

1. Open **Team & roles** → **Staff**. You see a card for each person.

   ![Your staff list.](../../../shots/spa/c08-s01-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. A therapist card.
   2. The numbers on the card: bookings **today**, today's **shift** and the **pay** type.
   3. **Not bookable**: this person (here a receptionist) does not take bookings.
   4. The document status (here **Documents valid**). It turns amber or red when a document expires soon or has expired (chapter 19).
   5. **Add therapist**.

2. Press **Add therapist**.
3. Under **Photo**, press **Choose from library** to pick a photo, or **Upload** a new one in the **Choose an image** window. Your website shows it when **Show on website** is on (step 12).
4. Type the **Display name**. The first name is enough. Clients see it.
5. Choose the **Gender**. Clients often ask for a female or male therapist.
6. Type the **UAE mobile**.
7. Optional: under **Team member login**, choose the person's login. They can then see their own schedule (the login is created by an invitation, chapter 9).
8. Choose the **Pay type**:
   - **Commission per booking**: the AED commission entered for each completed booking. No base salary, no percentage.
   - **Fixed monthly salary**: fill in **Base salary** in AED.
   - **% of sales**: only shown for staff who already have it. New staff can't get it. Their **Commission** is a percent of the sales lines credited to them, without VAT.
   - **Fee per booking created**: for receptionists. They earn the booking fee (set on the payroll page) for each booking they created that was completed.
9. Pick a **Colour on the calendar**.
10. Under **Skills**, tick the services this person performs.
11. Leave **Bookable** and **Active** ticked.
12. Tick **Show on website** if this person should appear on your website: "Name, photo and short bio appear in your website’s team section." Without a photo you see "Add a photo to show on HTML-design websites".
13. Optional: type a **Short bio for the website (English)** and a **Short bio for the website (Arabic, optional)**, up to 120 characters each, for example "Thai massage and deep tissue". Keep it short: some website designs show only its first 80 characters.
14. Press **Add therapist**.

   ![The therapist form.](../../../shots/spa/c08-s01-b.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Photo**, **Display name** and **Gender**.
   2. **Team member login**.
   3. **Pay type**.
   4. **Skills**.
   5. **Bookable**, **Active** and **Show on website**.
   6. The two short bios for the website.
   7. **Add therapist**.

> **Important:** Only the services ticked under **Skills** can be booked with this therapist. When you add a new service, tick it on the right therapists too.

> **Important:** **Show on website** starts off for new staff: nobody appears on your website until it is on. If your website already had a Team section before this switch came, the people it showed were switched on for you. Our studio may also switch it on for a therapist when it gives them a photo from your website's design. You can switch it off at any time. **Bookable** decides the calendar and online booking; **Show on website** decides the website. On websites with an HTML design, a person without a photo is not shown.

> **Tip:** People with **Show on website** on have an **On website** pill on their card and their page. Inactive or archived staff are taken off the website.

> **Tip:** Untick **Bookable** for staff who work at the spa but do not give treatments, such as a receptionist. They stay on the staff list for payroll and documents, but not on the calendar or the booking page.

To stop working with someone, archive them: open their form (**Edit profile** on their page) and press **Archive**. You see "Archive *name*? They'll no longer be bookable." Confirm it. Archived staff keep their history.

## Weekly schedule and shifts

Shifts say when a therapist works. Clients can only book a therapist during a shift.

1. On **Staff**, click a therapist to open their page.
2. Under **Weekly schedule**, choose **From** and **Until** (up to 92 days at a time) and the **Branch**.
3. Under **Working days**, tick the days they work. Set the start and end time for each day. Press **Copy first day to all** to save time.
4. Press **Generate shifts**. You see how many shifts were added, and how many were skipped because a shift already existed.
5. Check **Upcoming shifts**. It shows the next 14 days.
6. To delete a single shift, press its bin icon.

   ![A therapist's schedule page.](../../../shots/spa/c08-s02-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The therapist's name and **Edit profile**.
   2. **Skills**.
   3. **Weekly schedule**: **From**, **Until** and **Branch**.
   4. **Working days** and **Copy first day to all**.
   5. **Generate shifts**.
   6. **Upcoming shifts**.

> **Tip:** An end time before the start time runs past midnight. For example, 02:00 PM to 12:00 AM shows **next day**, and the shift shows **+1 day**.

> **Tip:** Days that already have an overlapping shift are skipped. You can safely press **Generate shifts** again for a longer period.

> **Important:** Shifts drive the walk-in turn list, occupancy and the dashboard numbers. Approved leave blocks bookings for that therapist (chapter 18).

If you see "Turn on at least one day", tick at least one working day. If you see "Up to 92 days at a time", choose a shorter period and repeat it later.
