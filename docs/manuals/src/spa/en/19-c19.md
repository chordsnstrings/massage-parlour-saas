# 19. Documents and expiry reminders

## What this is for

Keep your team's visas, Emirates IDs and health cards, and your trade licence and Ejari, in one place. The CRM reminds you before anything expires, so nobody works on an expired document.

## Add a document

1. Open **Team & roles** → **Documents** and press **Add document**.
2. Under **Belongs to**, choose **A staff member** or **The business**.
3. For a staff member, choose the person under **Staff member**.
4. Choose the **Document** type, for example **Visa / residence permit**.
5. Type the **Number** if you like.
6. Choose the **Issue date** and the **Expiry date**.
7. Under **Scan**, press **Upload scan or photo** and choose the file. JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF, up to 8 MB.
8. Add **Notes** if you like.
9. Press **Add document**. You see "Document added".

The document types are: **Passport**, **Visa / residence permit**, **Emirates ID**, **Labour card / work permit**, **Medical fitness certificate**, **Occupational / massage health card**, **Training certificate**, **Trade licence**, **Municipality / DED permit**, **Ejari / tenancy contract**, **Civil defence certificate** and **Insurance policy**.

> **Tip:** We remind you 60, 30 and 7 days before a document expires, and on the day itself.

## Track expiry

1. The four tiles at the top count your documents: **Expired**, **Within 30 days**, **Within 60 days** and **Up to date**. Press a tile to show only those documents.
2. Use **Belongs to** to show one person's documents, only **The business**, or **Everyone**. The count on the right shows how many are listed, for example "9 of 9".
3. Each row shows the document, the person (or **Business**) and the number, the expiry date and a status: **Expired**, **Due in 30 days**, **Due in 60 days**, **OK** or **No expiry**.
4. Press **View** to open the scan.
5. Press the pencil (**Edit document**) to change the details or replace the file.
6. Press the bin (**Delete document**), then **Confirm delete**, to remove it.
7. Press **Clear filters** to see everything again.

> **Important:** Managers get expiry alerts automatically, 60, 30 and 7 days before a staff document expires (automation **Document expiry alerts**, daily at 09:00, chapter 27). They arrive as notifications, also on your phone when notifications are on (chapter 5). The **Document expiry** card on the **Team** page also lists what expires soon.

> **Tip:** When you renew a visa or card, edit the document: type the new number and dates and upload the new scan. The reminders start again from the new expiry date.
