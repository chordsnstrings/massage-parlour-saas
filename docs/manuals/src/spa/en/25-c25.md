# 25. Marketing tools (Premium)

## What this is for

Bring clients back with WhatsApp campaigns to the right group, print booking QR posters for reception and partners, answer your Google reviews and prepare Instagram posts. Everything in this chapter is part of the Premium plan.

> **Important:** Campaign messages are not sent automatically. They wait in the WhatsApp queue, and your team sends each one with one tap (chapter 16).

## Segments: saved groups of clients

A segment is a saved group of clients, for example everyone who hasn't visited in 60 days. You can message it again and again. The count updates by itself.

1. Open **Inbox & follow-ups** → **Campaigns**, tab **Segments**, and press **New segment**.
2. Under **Start from a preset**, pick one: **Win back (no visit 60 days)**, **Birthday this month**, **Package expiring in 14 days**, **VIPs (spent AED 2,000+)** or **First-timers (1 visit)**. The conditions are filled in for you.
3. Under **Who should be in it**, adjust the **Conditions**. Clients must match every condition.
4. To add one, open **Add a condition…** and choose, for example, **Last visit more than N days ago**, **Visits at least N**, **Has booked a treatment**, **Total spend at least AED**, **Birthday in the next N days**, **Gender**, **Language**, **Has a tag** or **Package expiring within N days**. You can use up to 12 conditions.
5. Check the **Preview** on the right: how many clients match, and the first ten of them.
6. Type the **Segment name**.
7. Press **Save segment**, or **Save & write campaign** to go straight on to the message.

   ![Build a segment.](../../../shots/spa/c25-s01-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Start from a preset**.

> **Important:** Only clients who have visited, have a mobile number and can receive marketing are counted. Clients who opted out, are blocklisted or are tagged "no-marketing" are always left out.

## Write and queue a campaign

1. On the **Campaigns** tab, press **New campaign** (or use **Save & write campaign** from a segment).
2. Under **Audience**, type the **Campaign name**, for example "October win-back", and choose the **Segment**. You see how many clients **Will receive it**.
3. Under **Message**, write the **English message** and, if you like, the **Arabic message (optional)**. Each client gets the message in their own language. Arabic speakers get the English text if you leave Arabic empty.
4. Press a chip under **Insert a variable** to add it to your text: **{name}** (first name), **{spa}** (spa name), **{booking_link}** (booking link) or **{offer_code}** (offer code).
5. If you like, choose an **Offer code**. It fills **{offer_code}**. You create codes under **Packages & gifts** → **Promo codes** (chapter 15).
6. Check the **Preview**: how the message reads in WhatsApp for a client in each language.
7. Under **When**, choose **Now**, or **Schedule** and pick the **Date and time (Dubai)**.
8. Press **Queue *n* messages**. You see, for example, "15 messages added to the WhatsApp queue". Or press **Save draft** to finish it later.

At the chosen time, the messages appear in **WhatsApp** → **Due now** for your team to send (chapter 16).

Built-in limits keep your campaigns friendly:

- a message can be at most 700 characters;
- you can schedule at most 90 days ahead;
- a campaign reaches at most 500 clients ("Duplicate it next week to reach the rest.");
- clients already messaged by another campaign within 7 days are skipped;
- the offer code must still be valid on the send date.

## Campaign results

1. On the **Campaigns** tab, open a campaign. Its status is **Draft**, **Scheduled**, **Sending**, **Done** or **Archived**.
2. The tiles show **Recipients**, **Sent** (with how many were skipped), **Waiting** (still in the WhatsApp queue) and **Booked ≤ 14 days**.
3. The **Recipients** list shows each client, their mobile, the message status (**Waiting**, **Opened**, **Sent** or **Skipped**), when it was sent and whether they **Booked**.
4. While messages are still waiting, press **Open WhatsApp queue** to send them. The button shows only while messages are waiting; the campaign in the picture is **Done**, so it has none.
5. Press **Duplicate** to copy the campaign into a new draft.
6. Press **Archive** to put it away. Unsent messages are taken out of the WhatsApp queue. You find it again on the **Archived** tab, where **Restore** brings it back.

## QR posters and partners

1. Open **Growth** → **Marketing** → **QR posters**.
2. **Reception poster** is your own booking QR. Bookings through it show as "Online · QR code".
3. Choose the paper size, **A4** or **A5**, and press **Print poster**. Press **Booking link** to copy the link behind the QR.
4. To give a partner, such as a hotel concierge desk, their own tracked poster, press **Add partner** under **Partners**.
5. Type the **Partner name** (it is printed on the poster) and press **Add partner**. You see "Partner added".
6. On the partner's row, press **Show poster** to print their poster.
7. Press **Pause** to stop counting bookings for a partner, and **Resume** to start again. A paused partner's link still books, without the partner.
8. The table counts **Bookings**, **Last 30 days** and **Completed** for each poster, and the date of the **Latest** booking.

## Google reviews

1. Open **Growth** → **Reviews**.
2. The tiles show your **Google rating**, **New this month**, **Awaiting reply** and the **Response rate**.
3. Filter by rating (**All ratings**, 5 to 1 stars) and by status (**Any status**, **Needs reply**, **Approved**, **Posted**, **Failed**).
4. Getting reviews in:
   - With Google Business Profile connected (chapter 26), new reviews arrive every two hours.
   - Without it, press **Add review** and paste the **Reviewer**, the **Rating** and the **Review text**.
5. On a review, press **Draft with AI**. The AI writes a reply in your brand voice. Edit the **Reply** if you like (up to 2,000 characters).
6. Without Google connected: copy the reply into Google yourself, tick **Posted on Google** and press **Save**.
7. With Google connected: press **Approve**, then **Post reply**. The reply appears on Google.

> **Tip:** On **Autopilot** (chapter 26), the AI answers 4- and 5-star reviews by itself. Reviews with 1 to 3 stars always wait for your approval.

> **Tip:** To ask happy clients for a review, switch on **Review requests** in **Automations** (chapter 27). They are WhatsApp drafts your team sends.

## Instagram drafts (Social posts)

1. Open **Growth** → **Marketing** → **Social posts**. The page is called **Instagram drafts**.
2. The tiles show **Posts scheduled**, **Awaiting approval**, **Reviews synced** and **Booking clicks**. **Where bookings come from** shows your bookings of the last 30 days by source.
3. Under **New draft**, type an **Idea**, for example "Weekday afternoon offer on our 90-minute Thai massage", or leave it empty for a fresh suggestion.
4. Tick **Create an image** if you want one, and press **Draft post**. Images take about 15 seconds.
5. The draft appears below with its image and caption, marked **Awaiting approval**. Not happy with the wording? Draft again with a more precise idea.
6. Press **Format** to choose how it goes out: **Feed post**, **Reel**, **Story** or **Carousel**, and which images or videos it uses. Press **Save format**.
7. Choose a date and time under **Schedule for** and press **Approve**. The post is **Scheduled** and publishes automatically at that time.
8. To publish a scheduled post straight away, press **Publish to Instagram**.
9. Instagram not connected? Press **Copy caption**, post it yourself in the Instagram app, then press **Mark posted**.
10. With Google Business Profile connected, scheduled and published posts also show **Post to Google**.
