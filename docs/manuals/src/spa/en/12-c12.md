# 12. Online booking and the booking widget

## What this is for

Let clients book at any time of day: from your website, your Instagram link, a QR code or another website. Online bookings arrive in your calendar, and you confirm them on WhatsApp.

## How clients book online

1. Your booking page is your website address followed by **/book**, for example yourspa.spamanagement.co/book. The **Book** buttons on your website open it too. If your website's design has its own booking form, for example a design made just for your spa, that form sends you a booking request in **Enquiries** instead (chapter 17). Nothing is reserved until you add the booking yourself.
2. The client chooses a treatment (step **Treatment**).
3. In step **Date & time**, they choose a day, optionally a **Therapist** (or **No preference**), and a time.
4. In step **Your details**, they type **Your name**, **UAE mobile** and, if they like, **Notes (optional)**.
5. They press **Request booking**.
6. They pay at the spa. No card is needed online.

After sending, the client sees **Booking requested**: "Your time is held. Tap below to confirm on WhatsApp — we reply quickly." They can press **Confirm on WhatsApp** or **Add to calendar**. A returning client who is confirmed automatically (chapter 6) sees **Booking confirmed**, with **Message us on WhatsApp** and **Add to calendar**.

> **Tip:** The booking page is in English and Arabic. The client switches with **العربية** at the top right.

> **Important:** Only services marked **Bookable online** and therapists marked **Bookable** appear. The free times follow your opening hours, the shifts, free rooms and free equipment.

## Confirm online bookings

1. A new online booking shows as **Pending** on the calendar and in **Bookings**. You also get the notification "New online booking … — waiting for confirmation".
2. Open the booking on the calendar.
3. Check the source, for example **Online · Instagram**.
4. Press **Confirm**.
5. Send the confirmation from the **WhatsApp** messages page (chapter 16), or press **Chat** to write to the client.

> **Tip:** The number next to **Bookings** in the left menu shows how many bookings today wait for confirmation.

The website source tells you where the client found you: **Instagram**, **Google Business Profile**, **Google search**, **QR code**, **Facebook**, **TikTok**, **WhatsApp**, **Booking widget**, **Campaign link**, **Other websites** or **Direct** (typed the address).

## Add the booking widget to another website

Already have another website, for example a hotel page? The booking widget adds a button to it that opens your booking page in a pop-up.

1. Open **Settings** → **Booking widget**.
2. Under **Your code**, press **Copy**.
3. Give the code to the person who manages that website. They paste it just before the closing body tag on every page.
4. To adjust the button, change these parts of the code:
   - **data-lang**: **en** (English) or **ar** (Arabic, right to left);
   - **data-color**: the button colour, for example #5E7D6B;
   - **data-text**: the button label.
5. Press **Open booking page** to test what the pop-up shows.

   ![The booking widget code.](../../../shots/spa/c12-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Your code**, with **Copy**.
   2. **Options**: **data-lang**, **data-color** and **data-text**.

Bookings from the widget arrive as pending online bookings, like your website's.

> **Tip:** Visits through the widget appear as "widget" in your website analytics (chapter 23).
