# 14. Sales and the till

## What this is for

Record every payment taken at the till, print or WhatsApp the receipt, issue tax invoices, correct mistakes and close the day.

> **Important:** The CRM records payments. It never charges a card. Take the money with cash, your own card terminal or a bank transfer, then record it here.

## The Sales page

1. Open **Sales** in the left menu (**Workspace**).
2. Read today's numbers: **Revenue** (VAT included), **Sales**, **Average ticket** and **Tips**.
3. Use the arrow next to **Today** to see an earlier business day. If you have several branches, choose the branch at the top right.
4. **Today's sales** lists every sale with how it was paid and its total.
5. **By payment method** shows the totals per method, the refunds and the tips per therapist.
6. Click a sale to open its receipt. Press **New sale** or **Daily close** at the top right.

> **Tip:** A day without sales says "Check out a booking from the calendar, or start a new sale for a walk-in."

## Check out a booking

1. Open the booking on the calendar and press **Check out**.
2. The client comes **From the booking**, with the booked treatments under **Items**.
3. For each item, check the **Therapist**, the **Price** and any **Discount**.
4. To add a tip, press **Add tip** under **Tips**. Choose the **Therapist**, type the **Amount** and choose how it was **Paid by**. Tips come on top of the bill and are kept for the therapist.
5. Under **Payment**, choose the method (for example **Card terminal**) and the amount. For a card terminal you can type the slip number; for a bank transfer the transfer reference.
6. To pay with more than one method, press **Split payment** and add the second method.
7. Check **Remaining**. It must be **AED 0**.
8. Press **Complete sale · AED …**. You see "Sale #*number* recorded" and the receipt opens. Completing the sale also marks the booking **Completed**. Enter the therapist commission afterwards with **Open booking** → **Save commission** (chapter 11).

> **Tip:** A service with "Price on request" has no price yet. Type a price for it before you complete the sale.

If you see **Over by**, the payments are more than the total. Correct the amount.

## New sale for a walk-in, a product or a custom item

1. On **Sales**, press **New sale**.
2. Under **Client**, search by name or mobile, or press **New client**. For an anonymous walk-in, leave it empty.
3. Add items:
   - **Add a service…** for a treatment;
   - **Add a product…** for a retail product (the stock is shown next to each product);
   - **Custom item** for anything else, for example an aromatherapy oil that is not in your products.
4. Choose the therapist for each service. Add tips and the payment, as in "Check out a booking".
5. Press **Complete sale**.

   ![A new sale at the till.](../../../shots/spa/c14-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Client**: search, add, or leave as walk-in.
   2. **Add a service…**

> **Tip:** Selling a retail product lowers its stock and records its cost in your accounts automatically.

## Sell packages, memberships and gift cards, and use them

**To sell:**

1. In a sale, choose **Sell a package…** or **Sell / renew a membership…**.
2. For a gift card, press **Gift card**. A gift card line appears. In its name field ("Gift card — for whom?"), type who it is for, and type the amount under **Price**.

**To use them:**

1. **Package session:** on the client's service line, tick **Use a session from "*package*" · *n* left**.
2. **Membership session:** on the member's service line, tick **Use an included session from membership "*name*" · *n* left**. This is not ticked for you.
3. **Member discount:** on the member's other service lines, **Member discount −*x*% ("*name*")** is ticked for you. Untick it if the discount should not apply.
4. **Gift card payment:** under **Payment**, choose **Gift card**. Then type the gift card code (ABCD-EFGH) or scan the QR code on the voucher.

   ![Packages and gift cards at checkout.](../../../shots/spa/c14-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   2. **Sell a package…** and **Sell / renew a membership…**

> **Important:** Money paid in advance for packages, memberships and gift cards is held in your accounts until it is used. Chapter 15 explains how to set them up.

## Receipt: share, print, tax invoice

1. After **Complete sale**, the receipt opens. You can also open any sale from the list on **Sales**.
2. Press **Share on WhatsApp** to send the receipt from your WhatsApp. Or press **Print**.
3. Press **New sale** to start the next sale.

   ![A receipt.](../../../shots/spa/c14-s05-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The receipt (a simplified tax invoice) with the VAT.
   2. **Share on WhatsApp**.
   3. **Print**.
   4. **Full tax invoice**.
   5. **Corrections**: **Refund** and **Void sale**.

**For a VAT-registered business customer:**

1. On the receipt, press **Full tax invoice**.
2. Under **Customer billing details**, fill in the **Billing name**, the **Billing address** and the **Customer TRN** (15 digits).
3. Tick **Also save on the client's profile** to use the details again next time.
4. Press **Save billing details**.
5. Press **Print**.

   ![A full tax invoice.](../../../shots/spa/c14-s05-b.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Customer billing details**.
   2. **Customer TRN**.
   3. **Save billing details**.
   4. The tax invoice, in English and Arabic.

> **Important:** Your spa needs its own TRN for tax invoices. Without it you see "Your spa has no TRN yet. Add it in Settings → Spa profile before issuing tax invoices." (chapter 6). Leave the **Customer TRN** empty when the customer is not VAT registered.

> **Tip:** A voided sale has no tax invoice. **Share on WhatsApp** is hidden for roles that may not see phone numbers.

## Correct a sale: void or refund

Corrections are on the receipt, under **Corrections**. Each one needs a reason, and every change is logged.

**Void: for a mistake on a day that is still open.**

1. Open the receipt and press **Void sale**.
2. Type the **Reason**, for example "Rang up the wrong client".
3. Press **Void sale** to confirm. The sale drops out of today's totals.

**Refund: when you give money back.**

1. First give the money back to the client: cash, card terminal or bank transfer.
2. Open the receipt and press **Refund**. **Record a refund** opens.
3. Under **Items**, type the quantity coming back for each item. Or press **Refund everything**.
4. Check **To give back**.
5. Choose how it was **Paid back by**.
6. Type the **Reason**.
7. Press **Record refund**. You see "Refund of *amount* recorded".

   ![Record a refund after giving the money back.](../../../shots/spa/c14-s06-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The items and the quantity to refund.
   2. **To give back**.
   3. **Paid back by**.
   4. **Reason**.
   5. **Record refund**.

> **Important:** Void only works while the business day is not closed and nothing was refunded on the sale. After that, use a refund. Sales with packages, memberships or gift cards (sold, used or paid with) can't be voided either. Record a refund instead.

> **Tip:** For packages and gift cards, only the unused value can be refunded. A used item shows "Used — nothing unused to refund".

A refund puts products back in stock, reverses their cost and reduces the therapist's commission for the refunded part. Only roles with **Refunds and voids** (Owner and Manager) can correct sales.

## Daily close (Z-report)

1. At the end of the business day, press **Daily close** on **Sales**.
2. Check **Takings**: the revenue, the VAT, the payments by method, the refunds and the tips.
3. Check **Cash in (sales + tips − refunds)**: the cash that came into the drawer today.
4. Under **Count the drawer**, type the **Opening float (AED)**: the cash in the drawer when you opened.
5. Count the cash in the drawer now and type it in **Counted cash (AED)**.
6. Check **Expected cash** and **Variance**. **AED 0** means the drawer is balanced.
7. Explain any difference in **Notes**. The note stays on the record.
8. Press **Close the day**. The day is locked.

   ![Close the day.](../../../shots/spa/c14-s07-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Takings**.

   3. **Opening float (AED)**.

**Past closes** lists the earlier days with their revenue, expected cash, counted cash and variance. **Balanced** means no difference.

> **Important:** The business day ends at the time set in **Business day ends at** (chapter 6), for example 02:00. Sales after midnight still belong to the day before.

> **Tip:** Roles with the **Daily close** permission can close the day: the Owner, Manager and Receptionist roles.
