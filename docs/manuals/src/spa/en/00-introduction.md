# About this manual

## What this is for

This manual explains how to run your spa with the spamanagement.co CRM. It covers every screen you use as the owner, from the calendar and the till to staff, stock, accounts and your website's menu. Every task is written as short steps you can follow in order.

## Who this manual is for

This manual is for the **spa owner**: the person whose login has the **Owner** role. The owner can see and do everything in the spa's CRM, including the subscription and the team's roles.

Your managers, receptionists and therapists see fewer menu items, depending on their role (chapter 9). They can still use the chapters that cover their work.

You do not need technical knowledge.

## The three addresses

| Address | What it is | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| **app.spamanagement.co** | Your spa CRM. You and your team sign in here. | You and your team |
| **yourspa.spamanagement.co** | Your spa's website, with your menu and prices. Once you connect your own domain (for example www.yourspa.ae), the website moves there. | Your clients |
| **yourspa.spamanagement.co/book** | Your online booking page. Clients book here, and from the **Book** buttons on your website. | Your clients |

"yourspa" stands for your spa's own web address, which you choose when you apply (chapter 2). **spamanagement.co** itself is our website, where new spas read about the product and apply.

## How to read this manual

- Read **chapter 1** first. It explains the four golden rules and the two plans.
- For a new spa, follow **chapter 6** (first-day setup) next.
- After that, go straight to the chapter you need. Each chapter stands on its own.
- Each chapter starts with **What this is for**: two or three lines that say what the chapter helps you do.
- Steps are numbered. Do them in order.
- Words in **bold** are the exact labels you see on the screen: buttons, menu items, field names and badges.
- A screenshot follows the step it shows. The numbered circles on a screenshot match the numbered list under it.
- Boxes that start with **Tip:** give a useful shortcut. Boxes that start with **Important:** warn you about something that is easy to get wrong or cannot be undone.
- The **Glossary** at the end explains the words used in this manual.

### About the screenshots

- All screenshots come from a **demo copy** with a made-up spa, "Serenity Spa & Wellness", and made-up clients, staff and numbers. No real customer data is shown.
- Some web addresses in the screenshots end in **localhost** because the demo copy runs on a test computer. On the live platform they read **spamanagement.co**.
- The screenshots show the CRM in English. You can switch the CRM to Thai at any time (chapter 3); the screens look the same.
- Some pages, such as **Campaigns** or **Instagram**, are part of the Premium plan. On the Standard plan they are not in your menu (chapter 1).

## The four golden rules

1. **Payments are recorded, never charged.** You take the money with cash, your own card terminal or a bank transfer. The CRM records it.
2. **Customer messages are WhatsApp click-to-send only.** The CRM writes the message. A person opens it in WhatsApp and presses send, every time. There is no SMS and no customer email.
3. **The spamanagement.co studio designs and publishes your website.** You keep its menu, prices, team and contact details up to date in your dashboard.
4. **The only contact address is ask@spamanagement.co.**

## Getting help

- For questions about the CRM, your plan, your bill or this manual, write to **ask@spamanagement.co**. It is the only spamanagement.co email address.
- For a change to your website, use **Request a change** on the **Website** page (chapter 24). Our studio makes the change, publishes it and replies there. You can also write to ask@spamanagement.co.
- Chapter 31 answers the most common questions.

> **Important:** Never send a password, a two-step verification code or a backup code to anyone, including us. We will never ask for them.
