# 26. AI and Instagram automation (Premium)

## What this is for

AI helpers that answer Instagram messages and comments, draft posts and review replies, answer your questions about the spa and help fill quiet hours. They always use your voice and follow the rules you set. This is part of the Premium plan, with a monthly AI allowance.

> **Important:** The AI never sends WhatsApp messages. Anything for WhatsApp is a draft that your team sends with one tap (chapter 16).

## AI studio: your AI team

1. Open **Growth** → **Marketing** → **AI studio**. The page is called **Your AI team**.
2. Each helper has a card with its status: **Autopilot**, **Needs approval** or **Off**.
   - **Instagram receptionist**: answers DMs about prices, hours and location, and books appointments from chat.
   - **Instagram content**: drafts posts in English and Arabic with on-brand images, ready for your approval.
   - **Google review replies**: drafts warm, personal replies to every review.
   - **SEO**: writes page titles and descriptions so people nearby find your website.
   - **Slot filler**: spots quiet hours and prepares WhatsApp offers for past clients to fill them.
3. **Shortcuts** takes you to **Chat with your receptionist**, **Instagram drafts** and **Review replies**.
4. **This month** shows the AI usage against your plan's budget.

### Set how a helper works

1. Press **Configure** on a helper.
2. Tick **Switched on** to use it, or untick it to switch it off.
3. Choose the **Mode**:
   - **Draft — a person approves everything**;
   - **Autopilot — send within the rules**.
4. Type the **Tone**, for example "warm, calm and professional" (3 to 120 characters).
5. Type any **Extra rules**, for example "Always mention free parking. Never offer discounts." (up to 1,000 characters).
6. Press **Save**.

### Brand voice

1. In **Brand voice**, describe your **Voice**, for example "Warm, calm and welcoming. Short sentences. No medical claims." (10 to 600 characters).
2. Under **Always**, list what the AI should always do, one per line, for example "Use the client's first name".
3. Under **Never**, list what it must never do, one per line, for example "No discounts in public replies".
4. Press **Save voice**. You see "Brand voice saved". Every helper uses this voice.

> **Tip:** Start every helper in **Draft** mode. Check its work for a week or two. Switch to **Autopilot** only when you are happy with the answers.

## Try the AI receptionist

1. In **AI studio**, press **Try the receptionist**.
2. Type a message as a client would, for example "Do you have a Thai massage tomorrow evening?", and press the send arrow.
3. The receptionist answers from your live menu and real availability.
4. If it makes a booking, you see "Booking request … created — confirm it in the calendar". The booking appears as pending in your calendar (chapter 12).
5. Press **Reset** to start a new conversation.

## Instagram inbox

1. Open **Inbox & follow-ups** → **Instagram**. The page is called **Inbox**: your Instagram DMs and comments in one place.
2. The tiles count **Open**, **Unread** and **Flagged** conversations.
3. Choose **Open**, **Flagged**, **Closed** or **All**, and pick a conversation. Each one shows who is answering: **AI replying** or **Team replying**, and "Draft to approve" when the AI has written a reply for you.
4. For an AI draft ("AI draft — waiting for your approval"):
   - press **Approve & send** to send it as it is;
   - or change the text and press **Send edited reply**;
   - or press **Discard**.
5. Press **Take over** to answer yourself. Typing your own reply also takes the conversation over from the AI. Press **Give back to AI** to let the AI answer again.
6. Press the flag (**Flag for review**) to mark a conversation that needs a closer look; it then shows under **Flagged**. The AI also flags conversations it shouldn't handle. Press **Close** when it is done, or **Reopen** to answer again.
7. Press **Link client** to match the chat to a client. Type the **Name** and, if you know it, the **UAE mobile (optional)**. An existing client is found by mobile number, or by exact name when there is no number. Otherwise a new client is created.

> **Important:** Instagram allows replies within 24 hours of the client's last message. The conversation shows the time left, for example "Reply window 16 h 42 min left". After that, you can only reply from the Instagram app.

> **Important:** Replies to comments are public. They appear under your post. For a comment, you can also send one **Reply privately** (a DM to that person) within 7 days of the comment. Instagram allows only one per comment.

> **Tip:** If Instagram isn't connected yet, you see that replies are saved here but not sent. Connect Instagram in **Settings** → **Instagram & Google** (see below).

## Ask AI about your spa

1. Press **Ask AI** in the top bar, on any page.
2. Tap one of the suggestions under "Try one of these:", for example "How many bookings do we have tomorrow?", or type your own question (up to 500 characters) in **Ask about bookings, clients, sales or shifts…**.
3. Press the send arrow (**Ask**).
4. The answer comes from your own data. Ask AI only reads; it never changes anything. Where it helps, it adds links under **Open** to the right screen, for example **Calendar · 12 Oct**.
5. A WhatsApp link opens WhatsApp with the message ready. Nothing is sent until you press send.
6. Press **New chat** to start again.

### Receipt scanning

In **Accounts** → **Expenses** and in **Purchases**, **Scan receipt** reads a photo of a receipt and fills in the details for you (chapters 21 and 22). Always check them before saving.

### The monthly AI allowance

1. The plan card at the bottom of the left menu shows "AI allowance · …% used this month". **AI studio** → **This month** shows the same in figures.
2. Near the limit, a banner warns you: "AI budget …% used this month — AI pauses when it is reached. Contact us to raise it."
3. At 100%, AI pauses: "AI paused: monthly AI budget reached — contact us. AI restarts on the 1st."
4. To raise your allowance, email **ask@spamanagement.co**.

## Connect Instagram, Facebook and Google

1. Open **Settings** → **Instagram & Google**.
2. **Instagram**: press **Connect Instagram** and sign in with your professional (business or creator) Instagram account. No Facebook Page is needed. Once connected, the card shows the account, **Access** ("Renews automatically") and **DM & comment events** ("Subscribed"), with **Open inbox**, **Reconnect** and **Disconnect**.
3. **Facebook Page**: press **Connect Facebook**, sign in with Facebook and choose the Page linked to your Instagram account under "Which Page belongs to this spa?", then press **Use this Page**.
4. **Google Business Profile**: press **Connect Google** and sign in with the Google account that manages your spa's profile. Choose your location under "Which location is this spa?" and press **Use this location**. Press **Sync now** any time to fetch new reviews.
5. Once Google is connected, two more cards appear:
   - **Book button on Google**: press **Add Book button** to add a button to your Google listing that opens your booking page. Bookings from it show as Google in your booking sources.
   - **Google Search Console**: press **Send sitemap** to tell Google about your site's pages.
6. **AI tools via Meta MCP**: choose what the AI may read, draft and publish on Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp. Use the switches for **Instagram inbox**, **Instagram posts**, **Facebook Page** and **WhatsApp drafts**, and **Autopilot for public replies**. Press **Save AI tools**.
7. Under **Ask the AI**, type an **Instruction**, for example "Draft replies to new comments and a post about our hot-stone offer", and press **Run**. The AI can only do what the switches above allow, and public replies wait for your approval unless autopilot is on.
8. To remove a connection, press **Disconnect** on its card and confirm.
