# 29. Subscription and billing

## What this is for

See your spamanagement.co plan, your payment schedule, your invoices and your payments, and pay by card, bank transfer or cash. Only the Owner sees **Billing**.

## Your plan and payment schedule

1. Open **Finance** → **Billing**. The page is called **Subscription**.
2. The plan card (**Your subscription**) shows your plan, its price (excl. VAT), the payment plan, the setup fee, the renewal date and the current period. If you have a discount, you see "Your discount: …".
3. **Invoices** lists every invoice with its **Number**, **Description**, **Issued** and **Due** dates, **Total** and **Status** (for example **Issued** or **Paid**).
4. **How to pay** shows our bank details (see **Pay an invoice** below).
5. **Payment schedule** lists the setup fee and each payment, for example "Month 4 of 12", with its due date and invoice number:
   - **Paid** with a green dot: received;
   - **Must pay** with a red dot: still to pay;
   - **Balance due**: part of it is paid;
   - **Overdue**: the due date has passed.
6. **Payments received** lists every payment we received, with its **Date**, **Method**, **Reference** and **Amount**.

## Pay an invoice

### By card

1. Press **Pay invoice by card** on the plan card, or **Pay by card** next to an invoice.
2. You pay on a secure Stripe page.
3. Back on **Billing**, you see "Thanks — your card payment is processing. This page updates once Stripe confirms it."
4. When Stripe confirms, you see "Thank you — your card payment was received and the invoice is marked paid."

### By bank transfer

1. Under **How to pay**, use the **Bank**, **Account name**, **IBAN** and **SWIFT** shown.
2. Mention the invoice number (for example SM-2026-0017) in the transfer, so the payment is easy to match.
3. Your account manager marks the invoice paid when the money arrives.

### By cash

Pay your account manager. Press **Message us on WhatsApp** for help or to arrange it.

## If a payment is late

1. When an invoice is overdue, a red bar appears at the top of every page: "Please pay your invoice to avoid your account being paused." Press **Pay now** to go to **Billing**.
2. If it stays unpaid, the bar shows the date the dashboard becomes read-only: "Invoice overdue — the dashboard becomes read-only on … unless it is paid." You also get a notification.
3. After that date, the dashboard is read-only. You can still look at everything, but changes are blocked. Your website and online booking keep working.
4. As soon as the payment is received, full access returns. You get the notification "Full access restored".

> **Important:** Your clients can still book online while your dashboard is read-only. Pay the invoice quickly so your team can work normally again.

> **Important:** Our team can also pause your account for late payment. The bar then says "Your account is paused for late payment — the dashboard is read-only until your invoice is paid." A pause does not end by itself when you pay: our team resumes your account once your payment is in. If full access has not come back soon after you paid, write to **ask@spamanagement.co**.

> **Tip:** Questions about an invoice? Email **ask@spamanagement.co** or press **Message us on WhatsApp** on **Billing**.
