# 22. Accounts and VAT

## What this is for

Your profit and loss, your money balances and your VAT return, built automatically from sales, refunds, purchases and expenses. You only record expenses such as rent and DEWA, and close the books for periods that are finished.

> **Important:** Payments are recorded, never processed. The CRM does not move money. It records what was paid in cash, on your own card machine or by bank transfer.

## Overview: profit, money and VAT

1. Open **Finance** → **Accounts**, tab **Overview**.
2. Choose the **Month** at the top right with the arrows.
3. The four tiles show **Revenue**, **Expenses**, **Net profit** (with the margin) and **VAT due** (for the quarter). Revenue and expenses compare with last month.
4. **Profit & loss** lists revenue and expenses by account, down to **Net profit**. Press **Excel** to download it.
5. **Last 4 months** shows revenue per month, excluding VAT.
6. **Where the money went** shows expenses by category.
7. **Recent transactions** lists the latest sales, refunds, payroll and expenses, with how each was paid.
8. **VAT return · Q…** shows the figures for your FTA VAT return (Form 201), with the due date: **Taxable sales (box 1)**, **Output VAT**, **Recoverable input VAT** and **Net VAT due**.
9. **Money** shows your balances at the end of the month: **Cash on hand**, **Card terminal (not yet settled)**, **Bank**, **Gift cards outstanding**, **Prepaid packages & memberships**, **Tips owed to staff** and **Commissions owed**.

## Record an expense

1. Open the **Expenses** tab and press **Add expense**.
2. Have the receipt? Press **Scan receipt** to snap or upload it.
   - On Premium, a photo of the receipt is read and the details are filled in: "Receipt read — check the details before saving."
   - On Standard, or for a PDF, the file is attached to the expense and you fill in the details yourself.
3. Choose the **Date** and type the **Amount paid (AED)**: the total you paid.
4. Choose the **Category**, for example **Rent**, **Utilities (DEWA, internet)**, **Visas & licensing** or **Marketing**.
5. Type the **Supplier** (for example DEWA) and a **Note** if you like.
6. Choose **Paid with**: **Cash**, **Bank**, **Card** or **Paid by owner**.
7. Tick **Includes 5% VAT (on a tax invoice)** if the invoice shows 5% VAT you can recover.
8. Press **Record expense**. You see "Expense recorded".

Every expense lands in your profit and loss and your VAT return automatically. Press **View receipt** on an expense to see the attached file.

> **Tip:** Made a mistake? Press **Void** on the expense, then **Confirm void**. You see "Expense voided — a reversal was posted". Then record it again correctly.

## Journal

1. Open the **Journal** tab and choose the month.
2. Each entry shows where it came from (for example **Sale**, **Refund**, **Expense**, **Payroll** or **Stock received**), the date, and its lines: **Account**, **Debit** and **Credit**.
3. Press **Export Excel** to give the journal to your accountant.

   ![The journal.](../../../shots/spa/c22-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. An entry with its source (here **Refund**) and its account lines.
   2. **Debit**.
   3. **Credit**.
   4. **Export Excel**.

> **Important:** Entries are never edited or deleted. Mistakes are corrected with a reversal, so the history always stays complete.

## Close the books

When a month is finished and checked, lock it so nobody changes it by accident.

1. On the **Overview** tab, scroll down to **Close the books**.
2. Under **Close through**, choose the last day to lock, for example the last day of last month.
3. Press **Close period**. You see "Books closed through *date*".

   ![Lock a finished period.](../../../shots/spa/c22-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Closed through …**: the date the books are locked to now.
   2. **Close through**.
   3. **Close period**.

After closing, nobody can add or change entries on or before that date. Corrections go into the open period. If someone tries, they see "That date is in a closed period. Pick a date after the lock date."

> **Important:** You can only close days that are over. Check the month with your accountant before you close it. Every close is recorded in the audit log (chapter 30). Recording expenses and closing periods needs the permission "Record expenses and close periods" (chapter 9).
