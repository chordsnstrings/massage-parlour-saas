# 20. Payroll and WPS

## What this is for

One monthly run for salaries, commissions, tips and advances, plus the WPS salary file for your bank or exchange house. The figures come from your sales and bookings automatically. You check them, pay your team and finalise.

## Prepare and finalise the monthly payroll

1. Open **Finance** → **VAT & payroll**. The page is called **Payroll**.
2. Choose the month at the top right with the arrows, for example **October 2026**.
3. Press **Prepare payroll**. The CRM calculates salary, unpaid commission, tips and advances for every active team member. You see "Payroll prepared — review it, then finalise".
4. Check the four tiles: **Salaries**, **Commission**, **Tips** and **Net to pay** (with the number of people).
5. Check each person's line: **Salary**, **Commission**, **Tips**, **Advances**, **Worked** (hours from the time clock), **Unpaid leave** and **Net pay**.
6. The run stays a **Draft** until you finalise: "Draft — re-prepare after new sales, then finalise once paid." After new sales or refunds, press **Re-prepare**.
7. Pay everyone (bank transfer, WPS or cash).
8. Choose the **Paid on** date and press **Finalise**. You see "Payroll finalised and posted to the accounts".
9. Press **WPS file** to download the salary information file for your bank or exchange house.

> **Important:** Nothing is posted to your accounts until you finalise. Finalising marks commissions and advances as settled and records the total as paid from the bank. A finalised month shows "Finalised *date* — posted to the accounts." and cannot be prepared again.

> **Tip:** Therapist commission is the AED amount entered on each completed booking (chapter 11). Figures recalculate automatically from every posted sale, refund and tip. Nothing to re-key.

> **Tip:** Unpaid leave (chapter 18) is deducted from salaries automatically.

## Pay details and WPS employer details

Set these up once. The WPS file needs them.

### Your WPS employer details

1. Scroll down to **WPS employer details**. It says "Not set up yet." the first time.
2. Press **Set up WPS** (or **Edit** later).
3. Type the **MOHRE establishment ID** (13 digits), the **Employer bank routing code** (9 digits; ask your bank or exchange house) and the **Bank / exchange house**.
4. Press **Save**. You see "WPS details saved". The card then shows "Establishment … · routing …".

### Each team member's pay details

1. On a person's payroll line, press **Add IBAN**. When an IBAN is already saved, the button says **Pay details**.
2. Type the **MOHRE person code** (14 digits, from the labour card or work permit).
3. Type the **Labour card number**.
4. Type the **IBAN**: AE followed by 21 digits.
5. Type the **Bank routing code** (9 digits) and the **Bank / exchange house**.
6. Press **Save**. You see "Pay details saved".

> **Important:** Staff without an IBAN are left out of the WPS file. Their payroll line shows "Cash — no IBAN". Pay them in cash.

## Advances, tips payout and the receptionist booking fee

### Record a salary advance

1. Press **Advance** at the top right of the payroll page.
2. Choose the **Team member**.
3. Type the **Amount (AED)** and choose the **Date**.
4. Under **Paid from**, choose **Cash drawer** or **Bank**.
5. Add a **Note** if you like.
6. Press **Record advance**. You see "Advance recorded — it will be deducted in the next payroll".

   ![Record a salary advance.](../../../shots/spa/c20-s03-a.png)

The card **Open advances** lists advances that are not yet deducted. They are deducted in the next finalised payroll.

### Tips & advances payout

The card **Tips & advances payout** shows, for each therapist, the tips they received this month, the advances they took, and the **Net**: tips minus advances. Therapists are paid their booking commission in payroll. Tips and advances are settled here.

### Receptionist booking fee

Some receptionists are paid per booking. Their pay type is "Fee per booking created" (set under **Staff**, chapter 8).

1. In **Receptionist booking fee**, press **Edit**.
2. Type the **Fee per completed booking (AED)**.
3. Press **Save**. You see "Booking fee saved". The card then shows, for example, "AED 5 per completed booking".

They earn this fee for each booking they created that ended completed in the month.

> **Important:** Payroll records what you paid. It never sends money. You pay your team yourself, by bank, WPS or cash.
