# 6. First-day setup

## What this is for

Get your spa ready to take bookings, in the right order: your details, logo, hours, services, therapists, team and website. The **Get set up** list on the Dashboard tracks your progress.

## The "Get set up" checklist

1. On the **Dashboard**, find **Get set up**. It says how far you are, for example "2 of 6 done".
2. Work through the items in order. Each item opens the right page:
   - **Add your address and WhatsApp number** opens **Settings** (this chapter).
   - **Add services, rooms and prices** opens **Services & rooms** (chapter 7).
   - **Add therapists and their shifts** opens **Staff** (chapter 8).
   - **Invite your team** opens **Team** (chapter 9).
   - **Review your website** opens **Website** (chapter 24). It gets its tick when our studio has published your site.
   - **Take your first booking** opens the **Calendar** (chapter 10).
3. A done item gets a green tick. When everything is done, the list says "Setup complete — nice work."

> **Tip:** Already have a client list or a price list in Excel? Import it instead of typing everything (chapter 30).

## Spa profile and main branch

1. Open **Settings** (left menu, **System**). You start on the **Spa profile** tab.
2. In the **Spa profile** card, check the **Spa name**. Fill in the **Legal name** and, if you are VAT registered, the **TRN** (your 15-digit VAT number). The currency is always AED.
3. In the **Main branch** card, fill in:
   - **Branch name**;
   - **Business day ends at**: the time your business day closes (see the Important box below);
   - **Address**;
   - **Google Maps link** (optional);
   - **Area shown on the website**: the area your website shows, for example "Dubai Marina, Dubai" ("e.g. Al Barsha, Dubai. Empty = the city from the address.");
   - **Area in Arabic (optional)**: the same area for your Arabic website ("Shown on the Arabic website. Empty = the English one.");
   - **Phone**;
   - **WhatsApp number**: the number clients message.
4. Choose whether to **Show prices on the website**. This is the default for every service. A single service can override it.
5. Press **Save changes**. You see "Settings saved".

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The **Spa profile** card: **Spa name**, **Legal name**, **TRN** and **Currency**.
   2. The **Main branch** card.

> **Important:** **Business day ends at** decides which day late sales belong to. If you close at 02:00, set 02:00. A sale at 01:30 then counts for the day before, not for the new day. Reports and the daily close use this business day.

> **Important:** Without a TRN you cannot print full tax invoices. Add the TRN as soon as you are VAT registered.

> **Tip:** For the **Google Maps link**: open your shop in Google Maps, tap **Share** and paste the link. The address on your website then opens this exact pin.

> **Tip:** Services with hidden prices show "Price on request" on your website.

> **Tip:** When our team approves your application, the area starts as your emirate, for example Dubai. Make it more precise if you like. Only the main branch's area appears on your website, and only in website designs that show an area.

## Your logo

1. In **Settings** → **Spa profile**, find the **Logo** card.
2. Press **Choose File** and pick your logo. Use a square PNG, JPG or WebP file of up to 1 MB.
3. Press **Upload logo**. You see "Logo updated".
4. To remove the logo, press **Remove logo**. Your spa initials are shown instead.

   ![Upload your logo.](../../../shots/spa/c06-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. Your current logo.
   2. **Choose File**.
   3. **Upload logo**.
   4. **Remove logo**.

Your logo is shown at the top of the left menu. It is also the icon of the installed app (chapter 4).

> **Tip:** Square images work best. A wide logo is shrunk to fit the square.

## Opening hours

1. Open **Settings** → **Opening hours**.
2. Each branch has its own card. For each day, set the opening time and the closing time.
3. To close a day, press **×** next to its times. The day then shows **Closed**. Press **Open** to open it again.
4. For a split day (for example a break in the afternoon), press **Add** to add a second interval.
5. Press **Copy to all** on a day to copy its times to every day.
6. Press **Save hours**. You see "Opening hours saved".

   ![Set when clients can book.](../../../shots/spa/c06-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. A day with its opening and closing time.
   2. The note **closes after midnight**.
   3. **Add** (a second interval).
   4. **Copy to all**.
   5. **Save hours**.

> **Tip:** A closing time earlier than the opening time runs past midnight. For example, 10:00 AM to 02:00 AM means you close at 2 at night. The day then shows **closes after midnight**.

> **Important:** Until you save, the page says "Not set yet — showing the default (10:00–midnight daily). Save to confirm." Clients can only book within your opening hours.

If you see "Times overlap" or "Opening and closing times are the same", correct the times and save again.

## Online booking settings

1. In **Settings** → **Spa profile**, find the **Online booking** card. It covers bookings from your website, Instagram and the AI assistant.
2. Switch on **Auto-confirm returning clients** if you trust your regulars.
3. Set **After completed visits**: how many completed visits make a client "returning". You can choose from 1 to 50.
4. Press **Save online booking**. You see "Online booking settings saved".

   ![Decide which online bookings confirm themselves.](../../../shots/spa/c06-s05-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Auto-confirm returning clients**.
   2. **After completed visits**.
   3. **Save online booking**.

Returning clients (matched by their phone number) are confirmed at once. They get the WhatsApp confirmation and reminders. Everyone else waits until you confirm the booking (chapter 10).

> **Tip:** Leave auto-confirm off for the first weeks. You then see every online booking before it is confirmed.
