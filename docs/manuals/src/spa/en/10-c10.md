# 10. Calendar

## What this is for

See the day by therapist or by room, add bookings and walk-ins, and move each client through check-in, treatment and checkout. The calendar reserves the therapist, the room and any equipment together, so double bookings cannot happen.

## Reading the calendar

1. Open **Calendar** in the left menu (**Workspace**).
2. Choose **Day**, **Week** or **Month** at the top.
3. Move with the arrows, press **Today** to come back to today, or pick a date in the date box. The green pill (for example **Today · Sun 11 Oct**) shows when you are looking at today.
4. In the **Day** view, choose the columns: **Therapists** or **Rooms**.
5. If you have several branches, choose the **Branch**.
6. Tick **Show cancelled** to see cancelled bookings too. The number in brackets is how many there are.
7. Below the calendar, the numbers show **Booked** (shift hours filled), **Walk-ins** and **Late-night (after 20:00)**.

   ![The calendar, Day view by therapist.](../../../shots/spa/c10-s01-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Day**, **Week** and **Month**.
   2. The arrows and **Today**.
   3. **Therapists** or **Rooms** as columns.

   6. The numbers: **Booked**, **Walk-ins** and **Late-night (after 20:00)**.

How to read a booking block:

- A tick icon next to the time means the treatment is **Completed**.
- A dashed border with a clock icon means the booking is **Pending**: it waits for you to confirm it.
- A pulsing dot next to the time means the client is **In service**. The status is also written at the bottom of the block.
- A red warning triangle means the equipment the treatment needs is missing ("Equipment missing").
- A therapist on approved leave shows **On leave**.

The statuses of a booking are **Pending**, **Confirmed**, **Checked in**, **In service**, **Completed**, **No-show** and **Cancelled**.

8. In the **Week** and **Month** views, each day shows its bookings and their number. Click a day to open it in the **Day** view.

   3. Click a day to open it.

9. On a phone, the **Day** view is a list per therapist. Tap a name at the top to see one therapist, or **Everyone**.

> **Tip:** No columns? Add bookable therapists under **Staff** (chapter 8) and rooms under **Services & rooms** (chapter 7).

## Create a booking

1. Go to the day of the booking.
2. Press **New booking**, or click an empty slot in a therapist's or room's column.
3. Under **Client**, search by name or mobile. Or press **New client** and type the **Name** and the **UAE mobile**.
4. Choose the **Service**. Each choice shows the duration and the price.
5. Check the **Time** and choose the **Source**: **Phone** (the default), **WhatsApp**, **Walk-in** or **Instagram**. **Online** is set automatically for bookings made on your booking page.
6. Choose the **Therapist**, or **Any (automatic)**. Choose the **Room**, or **Automatic**.
7. Under **Notes**, add anything the therapist should know: pressure, focus areas, allergies.
8. Press **Create booking**.

   ![The New booking sheet.](../../../shots/spa/c10-s02-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Client**.
   2. **Service**.
   3. **Time** and **Source**. A booking started from an empty slot already has its time and therapist (here 17:00 with Nok).
   4. **Therapist** and **Room**.
   5. **Notes**.
   6. **Create booking**.

9. You see **Booking confirmed** with the booking reference.
10. Press **Send confirmation on WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with the message ready. Press send in WhatsApp.
11. Press **Done**.

   ![Send the confirmation from your own WhatsApp.](../../../shots/spa/c10-s02-b.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Booking confirmed**, with the reference (here **DFSLY**).
   2. **Send confirmation on WhatsApp**.

> **Important:** Nothing is sent automatically. The CRM writes the message and opens WhatsApp; a person presses send. A reminder is also prepared in the WhatsApp queue (chapter 16).

> **Important:** If you see "This client is on the blocklist.", check with a manager before you book (chapter 13).

If you see "*name* is not free right now", choose another time or another therapist. If the client has no mobile number, you see "No mobile number on file, so there's nothing to send."

## Walk-ins and the turn list

The **Walk-ins** card on the right of the calendar is today's turn list. It shares walk-ins fairly between the therapists on shift.

1. Check the **Walk-ins** card. Each therapist shows their status (for example **With client** or **Off shift**) and their place in the turn list.

   ![Today's walk-in turn list.](../../../shots/spa/c10-s03-b.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. A therapist's status (here **With client**).
   2. The therapist at the top of the turn list.

2. Press **Walk-in**.
3. Choose the **Service**.
4. Under **Therapist**, choose **Next in rotation (*name*)** or **First free therapist**. **Next in rotation** is offered only while a therapist is free.
5. If you like, type the client's **Name** and **UAE mobile**. They are optional, but useful for receipts and rebooking.
6. Press **Start walk-in**. The walk-in starts now. The room is assigned automatically and the client is checked in.

   ![Start a walk-in in a few taps.](../../../shots/spa/c10-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Service**.
   2. **Therapist** (here **First free therapist**: nobody was free when this picture was taken).
   3. The optional **Name** and **UAE mobile**.
   4. **Start walk-in**.

> **Tip:** If nobody is on shift, the card says "No one on shift". Add today's shifts first (chapter 8).

## Booking details: confirm, check in, start, no-show, cancel

1. Click a booking on the calendar. Its details open: the reference, the source, the service, the therapist, the room and the status.
2. Press the next step: **Confirm**, **Check in**, **Start service** or **No-show**.
3. To cancel, press **Cancel…**, type the **Cancellation reason** and press **Cancel booking**.
4. Press **Chat** to open WhatsApp with the client.
5. Press **Open booking** for the full booking page. You enter the commission there and can re-open a completed booking (chapter 11).
6. Press **Check out** to take the payment (chapter 14).

   ![Booking details with the next steps.](../../../shots/spa/c10-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The reference and the source (here **Ref YJVAC · Online · Direct**).
   2. The status (here **Confirmed**).
   3. The next steps: **Check in**, **Start service** and **No-show**.
   4. **Cancel…**
   5. **Reschedule**.
   6. **Open booking**.
   7. **Check out**.

The usual order is: **Pending** → **Confirmed** → **Checked in** → **In service** → **Completed**.

To complete a booking, press **Check out** and take the payment (the booking is then marked **Completed**), or press **Open booking**, enter the commission and press **Mark completed** (chapter 11). You can skip steps this way, for example go straight from **Confirmed** to **Completed** with **Check out** or with **Open booking** → **Mark completed**.

> **Tip:** A cancelled or no-show booking cannot be undone; the time has been released. Make a new booking instead (the time is checked again).

> **Important:** **Check out** is offered for every booking except cancelled ones and no-shows.

## Move or reschedule a booking

1. Drag a booking block to another time, or to another therapist's or room's column, and drop it.
2. Or open the booking and press **Reschedule**.
3. Choose the new **Date** and **Time**. Change the **Therapist** or **Room** if needed.
4. Press **Move booking**. You see "Booking *reference* moved to *time*".

   ![Reschedule a booking.](../../../shots/spa/c10-s05-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The new **Date**.
   2. The new **Time**.
   3. **Move booking**.

If the new slot is not free, the calendar refuses the move and you see "Could not move the booking".

> **Important:** Completed, cancelled and no-show bookings cannot be moved.

## What a therapist sees

1. A therapist who signs in with a login linked to their staff profile opens on their own home screen. It shows **Next client**, the **Today** list and **My earnings** (today, this week and this month: commission and tips).
2. In the calendar they see their own bookings. They can check in, start and complete them.
3. Commission is entered by the front desk after each treatment.

   ![The therapist's home screen.](../../../shots/spa/c10-s06-a.png)

   *In the screenshot (signed in as the therapist Nok):*

   1. **Next client**.
   2. **Today**: the rest of the day's bookings.
   3. **My earnings**.

> **Tip:** If the therapist sees "Your schedule isn't linked yet", link their login on their staff profile under **Team member login** (chapter 8).

> **Important:** Therapists never see client phone numbers. The menu shows them only what their role allows.
