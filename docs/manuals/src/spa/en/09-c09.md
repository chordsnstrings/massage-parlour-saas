# 9. Team, roles and permissions

## What this is for

Decide who can sign in to your spa and what each person can see and do. Six ready-made roles cover most spas. You can also add your own.

## Invite a team member

1. Open **Team & roles** → **Team** and press **Invite**.

   ![The Team page.](../../../shots/spa/c09-s01-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Members**: each person with their role, branches, status and joining date.
   2. **Pending invitations**, with **Revoke** on each one.
   3. **Invite**.
   4. **Roles**.

2. Type the person's **Email** and choose the **Role**.
3. Press **Create invitation**.
4. We email the link to the person. You can also press **Copy link**, or **Share on WhatsApp** to send it in a chat.

   ![Share the invitation.](../../../shots/spa/c09-s01-b.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Copy link**.
   2. **Share on WhatsApp**.

5. Until the person accepts, the invitation is listed under **Pending invitations**. Press **Revoke** to cancel it.

Chapter 2 shows what the person sees when they open the link.

> **Important:** Links expire after 7 days. If a link has expired, revoke it and send a new invitation.

> **Tip:** Only owners can invite another owner. If you see "This person is already on your team.", the email is already a member.

> **Tip:** A therapist with a login can see their own schedule. Link their login on their staff profile (chapter 8).

## Change a member's role, branches or access

1. On **Team**, press **Edit** on the member.
2. Change the **Role**.
3. Under **Access**, choose **Active**, or **Disabled — can't sign in to this spa**.
4. Under **Branches**, tick the branches they work in, or **All branches**.
5. Press **Save**.

   ![Edit a team member.](../../../shots/spa/c09-s02-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Role**.
   2. **Branches**.
   3. **Access**.
   4. **Save**.

> **Tip:** People only see the bookings, sales and messages of the branches ticked for them.

> **Important:** These rules protect your spa: only owners can change owners; you cannot disable yourself; your spa always needs at least one active owner.

## The six ready-made roles

1. On **Team**, the **Roles & permissions** card shows each role. It shows whether the role sees client phone numbers, accounts and settings.
2. Press **Roles** (or **Manage roles**) to see every permission of every role.

   ![Who can do what, at a glance.](../../../shots/spa/c09-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Role**.
   2. **Sees phone**.
   3. **Accounts**.
   4. **Settings**.
   5. **Manage roles**.

| Role | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full access, including the subscription and roles. |
| **Manager** | Runs the spa day to day. Everything except the subscription (**Billing**). |
| **Receptionist** | Bookings, walk-ins, payments, clients and WhatsApp. Also the daily close, the staff list, the clock-in kiosk, their own leave, restock counts and approving AI drafts. |
| **Therapist** | Their own schedule, check-in and check-out, and their earnings. Never sees client phone numbers. |
| **Accountant** | Accounts and reports. Also the dashboard with revenue, stock counts and purchases. |
| **Content editor** | Opens the **Website** page, uploads photos to **Media** and sends change requests to our studio. Sees services and prices but can't change them. |

> **Tip:** The ready-made roles are fixed. To change what a role can do, create a custom role (next section).

## Create a custom role

1. Open **Team** → **Roles** and press **New role**.
2. Type a **Name** and a **Description**.
3. Tick exactly what this role can do. The permissions are grouped, for example **Dashboard**, **Calendar & bookings**, **Clients**, **Point of sale**, **Services & rooms**, **Staff**, **Time clock & leave**, **Inventory**, **WhatsApp & marketing**, **Website**, **Reports & analytics**, **Accounting**, **AI agents**, **Team & roles**, **Business settings**, **Subscription** and **Audit log**.
4. Press **Save role**.

   ![Build a role with exactly the access you want.](../../../shots/spa/c09-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Name**.
   2. **Description**.
   3. A permission group with its checkboxes (here **Calendar & bookings**).
   4. **Save role**.

5. Give the role to people with **Edit** on the **Team** page.

To delete a custom role, first move everyone on it to another role. Otherwise you see "Move people off this role first."

> **Important:** Whatever **Website** permissions a role has (**Edit text and images**, **Edit design and layout** or **Publish**), only the spamanagement.co studio edits, designs and publishes your website. People with **Edit text and images**, for example the **Content editor**, can open the **Website** page, upload photos to **Media** and send change requests to the studio. People with **Manage services, rooms and resources** keep services and prices up to date (chapter 24).

> **Tip:** **Manage services, rooms and resources** and **Manage staff, shifts and pay** also let a person choose and upload photos in the service and therapist forms. No **Website** permission is needed.

> **Tip:** Each role name must be unique. A custom role keeps the name you typed, also in Thai.

## Who sees client phone numbers

1. Only the **Owner**, **Manager** and **Receptionist** roles see client phone numbers.
2. Everyone else sees numbers masked. Where a WhatsApp share button needs the number, they see "Ask a manager to share — phone numbers are hidden for your role."

> **Important:** This rule is fixed. No other role, and no custom role, can be given client phone numbers. If you try, you see "Client phone numbers are only for the owner, managers and receptionists." Therapists never see client phone numbers.
