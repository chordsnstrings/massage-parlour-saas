# 11. Bookings list, commission and the waitlist

## What this is for

Find any booking by date. Mark it **Completed** with the therapist's commission, or **Cancelled**. Keep a waitlist for days that are fully booked.

## Find bookings

1. Open **Bookings** in the left menu (**Workspace**).
2. Set **From** and **To**.
3. If you like, filter by **Status**, **Source**, **Therapist** or **Branch**. Tick **Commission missing** to see only completed bookings without commission.
4. Press **Apply**. Press **Reset** to clear the filters.
5. Click a booking reference to open the booking.

How to read the **Status** column:

- The first word is the booking's bookkeeping status: **Pending** (still open), **Completed**, **Cancelled** or **No-show**.
- After the dot you see where the client is on the calendar, for example **Pending · Checked in** or **Pending · In service**.

The **Source** column shows how the booking came in. For online bookings it also shows the website source, for example **Online · Instagram** or **Online · QR code**.

> **Tip:** Use **Commission missing** once a day, before you close the till. Payroll only pays therapists the commission entered on their bookings.

If nothing matches, you see "No bookings match — Try a wider date range or clear the filters."

## Complete a booking with commission, re-open or cancel

1. Open the booking: click its reference in the list, or press **Open booking** on the calendar.
2. Check the **Details** and the **Treatments**.
3. Under **Therapist commission**, type the commission in AED for each therapist and service.
4. Press **Mark completed**. If the booking is already completed, press **Save commission**.

   ![Enter commission and complete the booking.](../../../shots/spa/c11-s02-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Details** and **Treatments**.
   2. The commission for each therapist (here a couples massage with two therapists).
   3. **Mark completed**.
   4. **Mark cancelled**, with an optional **Reason**.

5. To cancel, type a **Reason (optional)** under **Mark booking** and press **Mark cancelled**. Confirm "Cancel this booking?".
6. To re-open a completed booking, press **Mark pending**. Confirm "Re-open this booking? Its commission is reversed." (This button only shows on a completed booking.)

> **Important:** Re-opening or cancelling a completed booking reverses its commission. The change is recorded as a correction and posted to the accounts. Nothing is deleted.

> **Important:** A cancelled booking cannot be re-opened. Book a new appointment instead.

> **Important:** A booking that was checked out cannot be marked pending or cancelled while its sale stands. You see "This booking was checked out — void or refund the sale first". Void or refund the sale first (chapter 14, "Correct a sale: void or refund"), then change the booking.

> **Tip:** If a booking was completed at checkout without commission, the page says "Completed without commission (e.g. at checkout) — enter it below." Type the amounts and press **Save commission**.

Only people whose role has **Enter therapist commission** can complete bookings here (chapter 9).

## The waitlist

The waitlist holds clients who want a treatment on a day that is fully booked. When a matching slot frees up, a WhatsApp message is prepared for you to send.

1. On **Bookings** or the **Calendar**, press **Waitlist**.
2. Press **Add to waitlist**.
3. Choose the **Branch**. Type the **Client name** and **Mobile**.
4. Choose the **Treatment** and the **Day**.
5. If the client has a time window, fill in **From** and **Until**. Leave them empty for any time that day.
6. Add **Notes** if you like, for example "Prefers Somchai".
7. Press **Add to waitlist**.

   ![The waitlist.](../../../shots/spa/c11-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The counts: **Waiting**, **Notified** and **Booked**.
   2. An entry with its time window.
   3. **Book**.
   4. **Add to waitlist**.

8. When a matching slot frees up, the entry changes to **Notified** and a message waits in the WhatsApp queue. Press **Open messages** and send it (chapter 16).
9. When the client says yes, press **Book** on the entry. Choose the length, the start time and the therapist, then press **Create booking**. The slot is checked again before the booking is made.
10. Press **Remove** to take an entry off the list.

> **Tip:** Use **Open** and **All** at the top of the list to show only open entries or every entry. With several branches, choose the branch first.

> **Tip:** If a client with this mobile number already exists, the CRM uses that client. You do not get a duplicate.
