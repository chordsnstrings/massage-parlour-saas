# 7. Services, rooms and equipment

## What this is for

Set up your treatment menu with durations and prices, the rooms where treatments happen, and equipment such as hot-stone kits. The calendar, online booking, the till and your website all use this menu.

All of it is on **Services & menu** → **Services & rooms**. The page has three parts: **Treatment menu**, **Rooms** and **Equipment**.

## Start from a sample UAE spa menu

1. Open **Services & menu** → **Services & rooms**.
2. On an empty spa you see **No services yet**. Press **Add a sample UAE spa menu**.
3. You see "Sample menu added — edit prices to match yours". Edit each service so the prices match yours (see "Add or edit a service").

   ![An empty menu with the sample-menu shortcut.](../../../shots/spa/c07-s01-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **No services yet**.
   2. **Add a sample UAE spa menu**.
   3. **Add service**, to add your own services one by one instead.

> **Important:** The sample menu only works for a spa without services. If you already have services, you see "You already have services — the sample menu is for empty spas."

> **Tip:** All prices are in AED and include 5% VAT.

## Categories

Categories group your services on the menu and on your website, for example Massage, Body treatments and Couples.

1. Press **Category** at the top right.
2. Type the **Name (English)** and the **Name (Arabic)**.
3. Press **Add category**.

   ![Add a menu category.](../../../shots/spa/c07-s02-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The name in English and in Arabic.
   2. **Add category**.

4. To rename or delete a category, press **Edit** (the pencil) next to its name.

> **Tip:** Deleting a category does not delete its services. They stay on the menu, without a category.

## Add or edit a service

1. Press **Add service** at the top right, or press **Edit** on a service.

   ![Your treatment menu.](../../../shots/spa/c07-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. A category heading (here **Massage**).
   2. A service with its durations and prices.
   3. The status (here **Live**).
   4. **Add service**.
   5. **Category**.

2. Fill in **Name (English)** and **Name (Arabic)**, and the two descriptions.
3. Under **Photo**, press **Choose from library** to pick a photo from your media library, or press **Upload** in the **Choose an image** window to add a new one. Once a photo is set, the button says **Replace**, next to **Remove**. The photo is shown on your booking page and website menu.
4. Choose a **Category**.
5. Under **Therapists**, choose **1 therapist** or **2 therapists (couples / four hands)**.
6. Under **Durations & prices**, type each duration in minutes and its price in AED. Press **Add duration** for another length, for example 60 and 90 minutes.
7. Set **Buffer before** (minutes to prepare the room) and **Buffer after** (cleanup and change of linen).
8. Under **Room types**, tick the room types this service needs. Leave all off to allow any room.
9. Under **Equipment needed**, tick the equipment it needs, for example **Hot stones**.
10. Pick a **Colour on the calendar**.
11. Tick **Bookable online** to show the service on your booking page. Keep **Active** ticked.
12. Choose the **Price on website**: **Spa default (Settings)**, **Show prices** or **Hide prices**.
13. Press **Save service** (or **Add service** for a new one).

   ![The service form.](../../../shots/spa/c07-s03-b.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The names in English and Arabic.
   2. **Durations & prices** and **Add duration**.
   3. **Buffer before** and **Buffer after**.
   4. **Room types**.
   5. **Equipment needed**.
   6. **Bookable online**, **Active** and **Price on website**.
   7. **Save service**.

The columns of the menu tell you more about each service:

- **Durations & prices**: each length and its price.
- **Booked (30 d)**: how often it was booked in the last 30 days.
- **Stock linked**: the products each treatment uses up (chapter 21).
- **Status**: **Live** (bookable online), **Not online** (only bookable by your team) or **Inactive** (hidden everywhere).

> **Tip:** Leave a price blank for "price on request". You then type the price at checkout.

> **Tip:** Your website takes each treatment live from here: its names and descriptions in English and Arabic, its durations, prices and photo. It shows only treatments that are **Active** and **Bookable online** and have at least one duration.

> **Tip:** If our studio builds your site from a design that brings its own treatments, it can add them to your menu. They arrive **Active** and **Bookable online**, with the design's durations, an English name only, no **Category**, no therapist and no price ("Price on request"). Open each one: add your prices, the **Name (Arabic)** and a **Category**, then tick it under **Skills** for the right therapists (chapter 8). Until then guests see it on your website but can't book it online. The studio can also give a treatment you already have the design's photo or English description. Your prices and durations never change.

> **Tip:** A duration can be from 10 to 480 minutes. Buffers can be from 0 to 120 minutes. The calendar keeps the buffer time free before and after each booking.

> **Important:** Only therapists who have the service in their **Skills** can be booked for it (chapter 8). Add the new service to the right therapists.

To delete a service, open it and press **Delete**. Past bookings keep their details.

## Rooms

Every booking reserves a room, so two clients never share one.

1. Scroll down to **Rooms** and press **Room**.
2. Choose the **Branch**.
3. Type the **Room name**, for example Room 1.
4. Choose the **Type**: **Single**, **Couple**, **VIP**, **Foot**, **Thai mat** or **Other**.
5. Leave **Active** ticked.
6. Press **Add room**.

   ![Add a treatment room.](../../../shots/spa/c07-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Branch**.
   2. **Room name**.
   3. **Type**.
   4. **Add room**.

To change a room later, press its pencil. Untick **Active** to stop a room being booked, for example while it is repaired.

> **Tip:** The room type decides which services can use the room. A couples massage that needs a **Couple** room is only ever placed in a couples suite.

> **Important:** The CRM blocks double bookings of rooms and therapists automatically. You do not need to check this yourself.

## Equipment

Equipment is for kits and machines that a treatment needs, such as hot-stone kits. Each booking reserves one free unit of every type it needs.

1. Scroll down to **Equipment** and press **Add equipment**.
2. Choose the **Branch**.
3. Type a **Name**, for example Stone kit 1, and a **Type**, for example Hot stones.
4. Press **Add equipment**.
5. Add one entry for each physical kit. Two kits of the same type mean two bookings with hot stones can run at the same time.
6. Open the services that use the kit and tick its type under **Equipment needed**.

   ![Equipment such as hot-stone kits.](../../../shots/spa/c07-s05-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. An equipment unit with its type (here **Stone kit 1**, **Hot stones**).
   2. **Add equipment**.

On the menu, a service that needs equipment shows a badge such as **Needs Hot stones**. When all kits are busy, the calendar warns "Equipment missing: *type*".

> **Tip:** Units of the same type are interchangeable. A service asks for a type, not for a particular kit.
