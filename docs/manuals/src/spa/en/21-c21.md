# 21. Inventory, purchases and warehouse

## What this is for

Track your oils, towels, linen and the products you sell: how much you have, what each treatment uses, what you buy and where it is stored. Every stock movement flows into your accounts automatically.

You find these pages under **Services & menu**: the tabs **Inventory**, **Purchases** and **Warehouse**.

## Products

1. Open **Services & menu** → **Inventory** and press **Add product**.
2. Choose the **Type**: **Consumable (oils, towels, linen)** or **Retail (sold to clients)**.
3. Type the **Name** and, if you like, the **Name (Arabic)**.
4. Type the **Unit**, for example ml, pcs or bottle.
5. Type the **SKU / barcode** if the product has one.
6. Type the **Cost per unit (AED, excl. VAT)**.
7. For retail products, type the **Selling price (AED, retail only)**.
8. Under **Warn me when stock falls to**, type the level at which you want a warning.
9. Press **Add product**. You see "Product added".
10. The list shows each product with **In stock**, **Cost** and **Price**. A product at or below its warning level shows **Low**, and you get a notification.

## Receive and count stock

### Receive stock

1. Press **Receive** on a product.
2. Type the **Quantity** and the **Total paid (AED)**.
3. Choose the **Date** and, under **Paid from**, **Cash** or **Bank**.
4. Tick **Includes 5% VAT (on a tax invoice)** if the supplier's invoice shows VAT you can recover.
5. Press **Receive stock**. The purchase is recorded in your accounts and the stock goes up.

### Count stock

1. Press **Count** on a product. The window shows what the system expects, for example "System says 317 pcs."
2. Under **Counted**, type what is actually on the shelf.
3. Type a **Reason**, for example "Monthly count", "Damaged" or "Expired".
4. Press **Save count**.

   ![Count stock.](../../../shots/spa/c21-s02-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

You then see "Count matches — nothing to adjust", "Stock increased by …" or "Stock reduced by …". The difference is posted in your accounts as an adjustment at cost.

The card **Recent movements** lists what came in and went out, for example "Used in treatment".

> **Important:** You cannot receive or count stock on a date in a closed accounting period (chapter 22). You see "That date is in a closed accounting period."

## Used per treatment

Tell the CRM what each treatment uses. It then deducts the stock for you.

1. Scroll down to **Used per treatment** and press **Add**.
2. Choose the **Treatment** (with its duration) and the **Product**.
3. Type the **Amount per treatment**. For example, a 60-minute Swedish massage uses 30 ml oil and 2 towels: add two lines.
4. Press **Save**. You see "Usage saved — stock is deducted when the treatment is completed".

   ![What each treatment uses.](../../../shots/spa/c21-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. A treatment and what it uses, here **Swedish massage · 60 min** with 30 ml **Sweet almond oil**.
   2. **Add**.

Stock is deducted when the booking is marked completed. To stop deducting a product, press the cross (**Remove**) on its line.

## Record a purchase

Use **Purchases** for everything you buy: oils, linen, cleaning supplies, materials and equipment.

1. Open **Services & menu** → **Purchases** and press **Record purchase**.
2. Have the receipt? Press **Scan receipt** to snap or upload it. On Premium, the details are read and filled in for you. Check them before saving.
3. Choose the **Date** and type the **Supplier**.
4. Choose the **Category**: **Materials**, **Cleaning supplies**, **Consumables**, **Equipment** or **Other**.
5. Under **Receive stock at**, choose where stock items go: the **Warehouse** or a branch.
6. Under **Items**, for each line choose a stock product (or **Not a stock item** and type a description), then type the quantity and the **Unit cost (AED, before VAT)**. Press **Add item** for another line.
7. Type the **VAT (AED)**, or press **5%** to work it out. The **Subtotal**, **VAT** and **Total** update.
8. Choose **Paid with**: **Cash**, **Bank**, **Card** or **Paid by owner**.
9. Type the **Invoice / reference no.** and **Notes** if you like.
10. Press **Save purchase**. You see "Purchase recorded".

Stock items are added to inventory. Other items are booked as expenses by category. The VAT is recorded as recoverable input VAT for your VAT return.

The page also shows **Spent in** *month*, **Recoverable VAT** and the number of **Purchases**, and a **By category** summary.

> **Important:** Payments are recorded only. The CRM never pays your suppliers. The date cannot be in the future or in a closed period.

> **Tip:** Made a mistake? Press **Void** on the purchase, then **Confirm void**. A reversal is posted; nothing is deleted. Then record it again correctly.

## Warehouse and transfers

The warehouse is central stock for the whole spa. Receive purchases there, then send stock to branches as they need it.

1. Open **Services & menu** → **Warehouse**. Each product shows what is in the **Warehouse** and what is **At branches**.
2. To move stock, press **Transfer** on a product.
3. Choose the **Direction**: **Warehouse → branch** or **Branch → warehouse**.
4. Choose the **Branch**.
5. Type the **Quantity**. Under the box you see how much is in the warehouse, for example "In the warehouse: 21,000 ml".
6. Add a **Note** if you like and press **Transfer**.

   ![Send stock to a branch.](../../../shots/spa/c21-s05-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Direction**.
   2. **Branch**.
   3. **Quantity**, with what is in the warehouse.
   4. **Transfer**.

Both sides are recorded, and the stock value stays the same. A message confirms how much was sent to the branch (or returned to the warehouse).

- Press **Count** to count warehouse stock. The difference is recorded and valued at cost.
- Press **Low level** to set a low-stock warning for the warehouse. Leave it empty to use the product's own level.
- **Warehouse activity** lists purchases, counts and transfers.
