# 28. Branches

## What this is for

Run several branches from one dashboard. Each branch has its own calendar, rooms, till, business day and opening hours. Your clients, packages and gift cards are shared by the whole spa. The Standard plan includes one branch; more branches need Premium.

## Add, edit or archive a branch

1. Open **Settings** → **Branches** and press **Add branch**.
2. Type the **Branch name** and the **Address**.
3. Paste the **Google Maps link** if you like. In Google Maps, open your shop, tap Share and paste the link. The address on your website then opens this exact pin.
4. Type the **Phone** and the **WhatsApp number** (a UAE mobile number).
5. Optional: type the **Area shown on the website**, for example "JLT, Dubai" ("e.g. Al Barsha, Dubai. Empty = the city from the address."), and the **Area in Arabic (optional)** ("Shown on the Arabic website. Empty = the English one.").
6. Set **Business day ends at**. For late-night hours, choose a time after midnight, for example 02:00. Sales until then count for the day before.
7. The **Time zone** is always Asia/Dubai (UAE).
8. Press **Save**. You see "Branch added".
9. The opening hours start as a copy of the main branch. Press **Opening hours** next to the branch to change them.

### Change or close a branch

1. Press the pencil next to a branch to edit it, then press **Save**. You see "Branch saved".
2. To close a branch, press the box (**Archive**).
3. Your browser asks you to confirm: "Archive *name*? Archived branches leave the calendar, till and online booking. Their history stays." Press **OK**. You see "Branch archived". It shows **Archived** in the list.
4. To open it again, press **Restore**.

> **Important:** Archived branches leave the calendar, till and online booking. Their history stays. The main branch can't be archived.

> **Tip:** Your website shows only the main branch's area. Change it here or in **Settings** → **Spa profile** (chapter 6).

> **Tip:** Give each team member access to the right branches on **Team** (chapter 9). Shifts are planned per branch (chapter 8).

> **Tip:** Packages, memberships and gift cards can be used at any branch (chapter 15).

## Branches and your plan

- The Standard plan includes one branch. The **Branches** tab only appears when you are on Premium or already have more than one branch.
- If you reach your plan's limit, you see "Your plan includes … branches — contact us to add more."
- To add more branches, ask your account manager at **ask@spamanagement.co** to move you to Premium.

> **Tip:** On many pages, for example **Time clock** and **Reports**, you choose the branch at the top right.
