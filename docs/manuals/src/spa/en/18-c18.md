# 18. Time clock and leave

## What this is for

Your team clocks in and out with a personal PIN on the reception tablet. You compare the hours they worked with their planned shifts, and you manage annual, sick and unpaid leave in the same place.

Open it from **Team & roles** → **Time clock**. The page has three tabs: **Kiosk**, **Timesheet** and **Leave**. If you have more than one branch, choose the branch at the top right, for example **Serenity Marina** or **Serenity JLT**.

## Set kiosk PINs

1. Open **Team & roles** → **Time clock**, tab **Kiosk**.
2. Scroll down to **Kiosk PINs**. Each person shows **PIN set** or **No PIN**.
3. Press **Set PIN** next to a person (or **Change PIN** if they already have one).
4. Type a **New PIN** of 4 to 8 digits and press **Save**. You see "PIN saved".
5. Tell the PIN to the person in private.
6. To remove a PIN, open it again, leave **New PIN** empty and save. You see "PIN removed".

   ![The kiosk and the PIN list.](../../../shots/spa/c18-s01-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The tabs **Kiosk**, **Timesheet** and **Leave**.
   2. **Tap your name**: one tile per team member, with **In since** a time, **Clocked out** or **No PIN yet**.
   3. **Kiosk PINs**.
   4. **Set PIN** for someone without a PIN.

> **Important:** PINs are stored hashed and never shown again, not even to you. If someone forgets their PIN, set a new one.

## Clock in and out

1. On the reception tablet, open **Time clock** → **Kiosk**.
2. The team member taps their own name under **Tap your name**.
3. They type their **PIN** and press **Clock in**. The kiosk shows "*name* clocked in at *time*", and their tile shows **In since** that time.
4. At the end of the shift they tap their name again, type the PIN and press **Clock out**. The kiosk shows how long they worked.

   ![Clock in with a PIN.](../../../shots/spa/c18-s02-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **PIN**.
   2. **Clock in**.

> **Tip:** A wrong PIN shows "Wrong PIN". After too many wrong PINs the kiosk locks that person for a few minutes: "Too many wrong PINs — try again in … minutes".

## Timesheet: actual vs planned

1. Open the **Timesheet** tab. The card **Actual vs planned** shows one week.
2. For each day and team member you see **Planned** (from their shifts), **Worked** (from the time clock), the **Difference** and the **Clock times**.
3. Use the arrows at the top right of the card to go to the **Previous week** or **Next week**.
4. Someone who is still clocked in shows "still in".

   ![The timesheet.](../../../shots/spa/c18-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Planned**.
   2. **Worked**.
   3. **Difference**: a plus when they worked longer than planned, a minus when they worked less (for example a missed day).
   4. **Previous week** and **Next week**.
   5. **Fix**.

### Correct a clock time

1. On the row, press **Fix**.
2. Change **Clock in** or **Clock out**. Times are Dubai time.
3. Leave **Clock out** empty while the person is still in.
4. Press **Save**. You see "Clock times saved".

> **Tip:** Worked hours count in payroll (column **Worked**, chapter 20). For team members without shifts, the time clock also gives the available hours in the RevPATH report (chapter 23).

## Leave requests

1. Open the **Leave** tab and press **Request leave** at the top right.
2. Choose the **Team member** and the **Type**: **Annual leave**, **Sick leave** or **Unpaid leave**.
3. Choose the **First day** and the **Last day**. Leave is in whole days.
4. Add a **Note** if you like, for example "Family visit".
5. Press **Send request**. You see "Leave requested". The request shows as **Pending**.
6. A manager presses **Approve** or **Reject**.
7. The person who asked can press **Withdraw** while the request is still pending.

   ![Leave requests.](../../../shots/spa/c18-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Request leave**.
   2. A pending request: the person, **Annual leave**, **Pending**, the dates, the number of days and the note.
   3. **Approve** and **Reject**.

> **Important:** Approved leave blocks bookings for that person, and the calendar shows them as **On leave**. If they already had bookings on those days, you see "Leave approved — … bookings need another therapist". Move those bookings to someone else (chapter 10).

> **Important:** Unpaid leave is deducted from salaries in payroll. The payroll shows it in the column **Unpaid leave** (chapter 20).

> **Tip:** A therapist with their own login can request leave for themselves. If their login is not linked to a team member, they see "Your login is not linked to a team member — ask a manager to request leave for you."
