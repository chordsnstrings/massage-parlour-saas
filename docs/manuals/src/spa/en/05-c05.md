# 5. Your account and security

## What this is for

Keep your login safe: your name, your password, two-step verification and notifications on your phone. These settings belong to you as a person, not to the spa. Every member of your team has their own.

## Profile and password

1. Open the profile menu (your name at the top of the left menu) and press **Account & security**.
2. Under **Profile**, change your **Name** and press **Save**.
3. Under **Password**, type your **Current password** and a **New password**.
4. Press **Update password**. You see "Password changed. Other devices were signed out."

   ![Account & security.](../../../shots/spa/c05-s01-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Profile**: your **Name** and **Save**.
   2. **Password**: **Current password**, **New password** and **Update password**.
   3. **Two-step verification**, with its status (here **On**).
   4. **Notifications on this device**.

> **Tip:** Press **My spas** in the dark menu on the left to go back to your spa list.

> **Important:** Changing your password signs you out on your other phones and computers. Sign in again there with the new password.

## Turn on two-step verification

With two-step verification, signing in needs your password and a 6-digit code from your phone. Someone who steals your password still cannot get in.

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone, for example Google Authenticator, 1Password or Authy.
2. On **Account & security**, under **Two-step verification**, type your password in **Confirm your password** and press **Set up**.
3. A QR code appears. Open the authenticator app and scan the code.
4. Type the **6-digit code** that the app shows.
5. Press **Verify & turn on**.

   ![Scan the code with your authenticator app.](../../../shots/spa/c05-s02-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The QR code to scan.
   2. The box for the **6-digit code**.
   3. **Verify & turn on**.

6. Your backup codes appear: "Save these backup codes somewhere safe. Each works once." Write them down or print them, and keep them safe.
7. Press **Done**. The badge now says **On**.

To switch it off, type your password in **Confirm your password** and press **Turn off**.

> **Important:** As the owner you can see everything in your spa: money, clients and staff. Your spa requires two-step verification for owners and managers, so keep it on for your login (last section of this chapter).

> **Tip:** Keep your backup codes away from your phone. If you lose your phone, a backup code is your way back in (chapter 2).

## Notifications on this device

You can get a notification on your phone or computer for new online bookings, document expiry reminders and your weekly insights.

1. On **Account & security**, go to **Notifications on this device**.
2. Press **Enable notifications**, then allow notifications when your browser asks.
3. Press **Send a test** to check that it works.
4. Repeat this on each phone or computer you use. Each device is set up separately.
5. To stop notifications on a device, press **Turn off**.

   ![The notifications card on this device.](../../../shots/spa/c05-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The status badge: **Off** at the top, **On** below, after you enable notifications.
   2. **Enable notifications**.
   3. **Send a test**.
   4. **Turn off**.

What the status badge means:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **On** | This device gets notifications. |
| **Off** | Press **Enable notifications** to switch them on. |
| **Blocked** | Notifications are blocked for this site. Allow them in your browser's site settings, then come back. |
| **Unavailable** | This browser cannot get notifications. On an iPhone, install the app first (chapter 4) and open it from the Home Screen. |
| **Not set up** | Push notifications are not switched on for the platform yet. Ask ask@spamanagement.co. |

> **Tip:** Even without phone notifications, everything also appears under the bell in the top bar (chapter 3).

## Two-step verification for owners and managers (on by default)

Your spa requires two-step verification for every owner and manager. This rule is switched on for every spa from the start.

1. An owner or manager who has no authenticator app yet is sent to **Account & security** before the dashboard opens.
2. They see: "Your spa requires two-step verification for your role. Set it up below, then open the dashboard again."
3. They set it up as described above, then open the dashboard again.

To check or change the rule:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Spa profile** and find the **Security** card.
2. **Require 2FA for owner & managers** is switched on.
3. To turn the rule off, switch it off and press **Save security**.
4. To turn it back on, switch it on and press **Save security**. This works only while your own two-step verification is on.

   ![The Security card in Settings.](../../../shots/spa/c05-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Two-step verification**: your own status (here **On for your sign-in**).
   2. **Require 2FA for owner & managers**, switched on.
   3. **Save security**.
   4. **Recent activity**: the latest changes in your spa.
   5. **View full audit log** (chapter 30).

> **Important:** If your own two-step verification is off, turning the rule back on shows "Turn on two-step verification for your own account first." This stops you from locking yourself out.

> **Tip:** We recommend that you keep the rule on. Owners and managers can see money, clients and staff.
