# 24. Your website

## What this is for

Your website is designed, written and published by the spamanagement.co studio. Your menu and prices, your team, opening hours and contact details come from your dashboard and go live straight away. On the **Website** page you see your site, keep its services and prices up to date, and ask our studio for any other change. This chapter also covers your photo library and your own domain.

## Who does what

1. Our studio builds your site. It picks a design for your spa (one of our templates, or a design made just for you), writes the texts in English and Arabic, and publishes the site. Every later design change is also made and published by the studio.
2. Our studio can draft the first texts with the help of AI, from your spa's own details: your spa name, your services and prices, your branches, your opening hours and your contact details. Fill these in first (chapter 6), so your site starts with the right facts.
3. Nothing goes live until our studio publishes it. A person in our studio reads and adjusts every text before your site is published.
4. Your menu with prices, your team and your opening hours come live from your dashboard on sites built from our templates and on HTML designs our studio has connected to your dashboard. On such an HTML design, your spa name, logo, treatment photos and descriptions, address, map pin, phone, WhatsApp and area name come live too. No publishing is needed. A design that is not connected yet shows its own fixed texts: if a change you make here does not appear on your site, tell our studio with **Request a change**. If a text on your site also mentions one of them, for example a price or your hours, ask the studio to update it the same way.
5. For anything else, for example new photos, a seasonal offer or different wording, use **Request a change** on the **Website** page. Our studio makes the change, publishes it and replies on the same page.

> **Important:** Only our studio team can change the website design or publish it. You change your services, prices, team and contact details yourself, in your dashboard. You don't need to review or approve your site: our studio publishes it for you.

Your website is in English and Arabic.

## The Website page

1. Open **Growth** → **Website studio** → **Website**.
2. The page has four parts: **Your website**, **Services & prices**, **Request a change** and **Your requests**.
3. **Your website** shows a preview of your site and its address. The pill says **Live** when your site is published.
4. Press **View live site** to open your website in a new tab. Press **Copy link** to copy its address, for example to share it on Instagram. Both buttons show once your site is live.

## Update services and prices on your website

1. On the **Website** page, find **Services & prices**. It lists what your website shows, by category, with the durations and prices of each service.
2. Each service shows **On the website** or **Not on the website**. A service is on the website when it is active, bookable online and has at least one duration.
3. Press **Edit** on a service.
4. Change its **Name (English)**, **Name (Arabic)**, **Description (English)**, **Description (Arabic)** or its **Durations & prices**. You can also choose its **Price on website**.
5. Press **Save service**. The change shows on your live site straight away. No publishing is needed.
6. For photos, rooms, buffers and online booking, press **All service settings**. It opens **Services & rooms** (chapter 7).

## What your website takes from your dashboard

On templates and connected HTML designs, your website reads these straight from your dashboard. Change them there, and your live site shows the change on its next page load.

| What visitors see | Where you change it | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| Your spa name and logo | **Settings** → **Spa profile**, and its **Logo** card | 6 |
| Treatments: names, descriptions, durations, prices and photos | **Services & menu** → **Services & rooms**. Only services that are **Active** and **Bookable online** and have a duration are shown | 7 |
| Therapists | **Team & roles** → **Staff**: people with **Show on website**, with their photo and short bio. HTML designs show only people with a photo | 8 |
| Address, map pin, phone, WhatsApp and area name | **Settings** → **Spa profile** (main branch), or **Settings** → **Branches** | 6, 28 |
| Opening hours | **Settings** → **Opening hours** | 6 |
| Prices shown or hidden | **Show prices on the website** in **Settings**, and **Price on website** on each service | 6, 7 |

Everything else, for example the texts, the design's own pictures and the page titles, goes through **Request a change** (see below).

> **Tip:** If something is empty in your dashboard, for example no logo, no opening hours or nobody with **Show on website**, your website hides that part. It never shows another spa's sample details. On a design made just for your spa, the design's own value stays instead.

> **Tip:** Some designs are laid out for English. Your Arabic website then shows the Arabic texts in the same layout.

> **Tip:** You can upload treatment and therapist photos straight in their forms (chapters 7 and 8).

> **Tip:** A design with its own booking form sends you booking requests instead of bookings (chapter 17).

> **Tip:** The preview on the **Website** page shows your live data. Its booking form never sends a request to your **Enquiries**. Some designs open WhatsApp instead.

## Ask the studio for a change

For anything your dashboard doesn't change, for example new photos, a seasonal offer, an opening-hours banner or different wording, ask our studio here.

1. On the **Website** page, find **Request a change**.
2. Under **Page**, choose the page to change, or **Whole site**.
3. Under **What should change?**, write what you would like, in a few clear sentences. For example: "Please add a banner at the top of the home page: from 1 November we open on Fridays from 14:00 to 02:00."
4. Press **Send to studio**. You see "Sent to our studio", and the form is empty again.

   1. **Page**: here **Home**. Choose **Whole site** for a change on every page.

> **Tip:** New photos? Upload them to **Media** first (see "Media library" below), then mention them in your request, for example by their name or tag.

> **Important:** While your dashboard is read-only because of an unpaid invoice (chapter 29), you cannot send new requests.

## Media library

Keep the photos for your website and posts in one place.

1. Open **Website studio** → **Media**.
2. Drop photos onto **Drop images here**, or press **Choose files**. JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF or GIF, up to 20 MB each.
3. Use **All**, **Uploads** or **AI images** to filter, or type in **Search name, alt text or tag**. Press a tag, for example **#rooms**, to show only those images.
4. Click an image to open its details.
5. Type the **Alt text (English)** and **Alt text (Arabic)**: a short description for screen readers and Google, for example "Warm stones on a towel".
6. Type **Tags**, separated by commas, for example rooms, team, offers. Press **Save details**.
7. In the details you can also press **Copy URL**, **Open** or **Delete**.

   ![Your photo library.](../../../shots/spa/c24-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Drop images here**, with **Choose files**.
   2. **All**, **Uploads**, **AI images** and the search box.
   3. An image.
   4. The tags.

Uploads are resized, stripped of location data and served fast from your site.

> **Important:** Before you delete an image, the CRM tells you where it is used, for example "Used on 1 page: Home. Deleting it will leave an empty image there." Press **Delete anyway** only if you are sure.

> **Tip:** Pictures our studio takes from your website's design are in **Media** too. Their tag starts with **design:**.

> **Tip:** An AI image that still lives on a temporary link shows **Temporary**. The link expires 7 days after it was made. Press **Save to library** to keep it.

## Connect your own domain

Your free address, for example yourspa.spamanagement.co, always works. If you own a domain, you can use it instead.

1. Open **Settings** → **Domains**.
2. Under **Connect your own domain**, type **Your domain**. We recommend the www version, for example www.yourspa.ae.
3. Press **Add domain**. You see "*domain* added — now add the two DNS records". The domain shows **Pending**.
4. Sign in where you bought the domain (your registrar), or wherever its DNS is managed. Look for "DNS", "Zone editor" or "Manage records".
5. Under **Set up DNS**, add the two records shown. Use **Copy** next to each **Name** and **Value**:
   - a **TXT** record: proves you own the domain;
   - a **CNAME** record: points the domain at your site.
6. Leave TTL on its default. Most providers add your domain to the name for you, so enter the short name shown.
7. Press **Check now**. The status goes **Pending** → **Verifying** → **Active**.
8. When the domain is **Active**, it becomes your primary address automatically (it shows **Primary address**), and your free address sends visitors to it. To go back to the free address, press **Make primary** on the **Free address** card. To use your own domain again, press **Make primary** on your domain.
9. Also want the bare domain (yourspa.ae, without www) to work? At your registrar, set up domain forwarding from yourspa.ae to https://www.yourspa.ae.

   ![Connect your own domain.](../../../shots/spa/c24-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Free address**, here **Primary**.
   2. Your domain. At the bottom: **Your domain** and **Add domain**, as you see them before you add one.
   3. The status, here **Pending**.
   4. **Set up DNS**: the **TXT** and **CNAME** records, each with **Copy**.
   5. **Check now**.

> **Important:** DNS changes usually show up within minutes, but can take up to 48 hours. Keep both records in place after your domain connects.

> **Tip:** You don't have to keep pressing **Check now**. We check a new domain automatically every 10 minutes, then every hour, and an active domain once a day.

> **Tip:** Is your DNS on Cloudflare? Set the CNAME record to "DNS only" (the grey cloud).

> **Tip:** The secure connection (SSL, https) is set up automatically once your DNS points at us. You may see the note "Automatic SSL is not switched on yet". You can ignore it: there is nothing to wait for. If https doesn't work an hour after the domain is **Active**, write to **ask@spamanagement.co**.

To stop using a domain, press **Remove**. You see "Remove *domain*? Your site will stop answering on this address. You can add it again later."

## Buy a domain through us

Don't have a domain yet? We can buy one for you and connect it.

1. On **Domains**, under **Buy a domain**, type a name in **Search for a name** and press **Search**.
2. Each available name shows its price per year. Names you can't buy show **Taken** or **Unavailable**.
3. Press **Request** next to the name you want, and confirm the yearly price.
4. Follow your request under **Domain requests**: **Awaiting approval**, then **Buying**, then **Bought**. Once it is registered, it connects automatically within an hour.
5. To withdraw a request that is still **Awaiting approval**, press **Cancel**.

   ![Search and request a domain.](../../../shots/spa/c24-s05-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Search for a name** and **Search**.
   2. The price per year.
   3. **Request**.

We register the domain in your spa's name, point it at your website and switch on SSL. The yearly registrar price is added to your next invoice.

> **Important:** .ae names aren't available here. Buy those from a UAE registrar and connect them under **Connect your own domain**.

> **Tip:** If you see "Buying domains isn't switched on yet", contact **ask@spamanagement.co**, or connect a domain you already own.
