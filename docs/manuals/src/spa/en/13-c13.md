# 13. Clients

## What this is for

Everyone who has booked or walked in has a profile: their visits, spend, preferences, treatment notes and signed intake forms, all in one place.

## The client list

1. Open **Clients** in the left menu (**People**).
2. The numbers at the top show **Total clients**, **Active packages**, **Birthdays this month** and **Win-back targets** (clients who have not visited for 60 days or more).
3. Search by name or phone in **Search name or phone**.
4. Or press a tag to filter the list, for example **vip** or **regular**. Press **All** to see everyone.
5. Click a client (or **View**) to open the profile.

   ![Your client list.](../../../shots/spa/c13-s01-a.png)

   1. The numbers: **Total clients**, **Active packages**, **Birthdays this month** and **Win-back targets**.

> **Tip:** For a very long list, the page shows the first clients only ("Showing the first … Search to narrow down."). Use the search box to find anyone.

> **Important:** Therapists never see client phone numbers. Only the front desk and managers do. Numbers are masked in every other role (chapter 9).

## Add or edit a client

1. Press **New client**, or press **Edit** on a client profile.
2. Type the **Name** and the **UAE mobile**. The mobile is used for WhatsApp, for example 050 123 4567.
3. If you like, fill in the **Gender**, the **Language** and the **Birthday**. Intake forms open in the client's language: English or Arabic (العربية).
4. Under **Tags**, type tags separated by commas, for example vip, regular.
5. Press **Add client** (or **Save** when you edit).

   ![Add a client.](../../../shots/spa/c13-s02-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Name**, **UAE mobile** and **Gender**.
   2. **Language**.
   3. **Tags**.
   4. **Add client**.

When you edit a client, you can also fill in **Email**, **Nationality** and **Notes**.

> **Tip:** The tag **no-marketing** keeps a client out of every campaign (chapter 25).

If you see "Another client already has this number.", the client is already in your list. Search for them instead.

## The client profile

1. At the top you see the client's **Visits**, **Spend**, **No-shows** and **Last visit**.
2. Press **WhatsApp** to open a chat with the client. Press **Sign intake** for the intake form. Press **Edit** to change their details.
3. **Visit history** lists every booking, with its status, price and reference.
4. **Treatment notes** are written by therapists after each session. Type the note under **New note**, link it to a visit under **Visit** if you like, and press **Add note**.
5. **Preferences** tell the therapist what to know before the session: **Pressure**, **Oils**, **Allergies**, **Focus areas**, **Therapist gender** and **Preferred therapist**. Press **Edit** on the card to change them, then save.
6. **Intake & waiver** lists the forms the client has signed.

Clients with a membership also have a **Memberships** card on the profile. It shows the period, the discount and the included sessions left. A client's package and its sessions left show in the **Package** column of the client list and at checkout. Memberships are sold and renewed at checkout, and their benefits apply there automatically (chapter 15).

> **Tip:** Allergies show in red, so nobody misses them.

## Blocklist a client

1. On the profile, find the **Blocklist** card and press **Blocklist client**.
2. Type the **Reason**, for example "Repeated no-shows". Everyone on your team can see it.
3. Press **Blocklist**.

   ![Blocklist a client.](../../../shots/spa/c13-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Reason**.
   2. **Blocklist**.

From now on, the team sees a warning whenever this client books: "This client is on the blocklist." To undo it, press **Remove from blocklist**. The client can then book again as normal.

> **Important:** Blocklisted clients are always left out of campaigns.

## Set up your intake form and waiver

The intake form holds the health questions and the consent that clients sign on the reception tablet before their first treatment.

1. Open **Settings** → **Intake & waiver**, or press **Intake form** on **Clients**.
2. Press **Use recommended template** for a ready-made UAE massage intake. Or press **Add question** to build your own.
3. For each question, fill in:
   - **Label (English)** and **Label (Arabic)**: the question;
   - **Answer type**: **Yes / no**, **Short answer**, **Long answer** or **Choice**;
   - **Key**: a short internal name for the answer, for example pregnant;
   - **Required**: tick it if the client must answer;
   - **Options**: for a **Choice** question, the choices separated by commas, for example Light, Medium, Firm.
4. Use the arrows to change the order of the questions. Press the bin to delete one.
5. Under **Form & waiver**, type the **Form name**, the **Waiver (English)** and the **Waiver (Arabic)**. The waiver is shown above the signature box.
6. Press **Save new version**. You see "Saved as version *n*".

   ![Your intake form and waiver.](../../../shots/spa/c13-s05-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The version (here **Version 2**).
   2. A question with its English and Arabic labels.
   3. **Answer type**.
   4. **Required**.
   5. **Waiver (English)** and **Waiver (Arabic)**.
   6. **Use recommended template**.

> **Important:** Saving creates a new version. Earlier signatures keep the exact questions and waiver they signed. Clients who signed an older version show **Re-sign needed** on their profile.

> **Tip:** An empty Arabic label falls back to the English one. Fill in both for Arabic-speaking clients.

## Sign an intake form on the tablet

1. Open the client's profile and press **Sign intake**.
2. Check the form language at the top right (**English** or **العربية**). It starts in the client's language.
3. Hand the tablet to the client.
4. The client answers the questions, ticks **I have read and accept the above**, signs with a finger under **Signature** and presses **Sign and submit**. **Clear** wipes the signature to try again.

   ![The client signs on the reception tablet.](../../../shots/spa/c13-s06-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The form language.
   2. The questions.
   3. **I have read and accept the above**.
   4. **Signature**, with **Clear**.
   5. **Sign and submit**.

5. The signed form is stored as a PDF. To see it, open the form under **Intake & waiver** on the client's profile.
6. Press **Print** or **Download PDF**.

   ![A signed intake form.](../../../shots/spa/c13-s06-b.png)

   2. **Print**.

> **Tip:** If no PDF was stored, press **Create PDF** (or **Regenerate PDF**) to make it again.

> **Tip:** You can download every signed PDF at once as a .zip file (chapter 30).

## Merge duplicate clients

Sometimes the same person is in your list twice, for example "Sarah Johnson" and "Sarah J." with the same mobile.

1. On **Clients**, press **Duplicates**.
2. Each pair shows why it looks like the same person: **Same mobile** or **Same name**. Press **Review**.
3. Check which client is the **Kept client** and which is **Merged and deleted**. Press **Keep the other one** to swap them.
4. Check **What moves** and **After the merge**.
5. Press **Merge clients** and confirm.

   ![Review and merge a duplicate.](../../../shots/spa/c13-s07-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Kept client**.
   2. **Merged and deleted**.
   3. **Keep the other one**.
   4. **What moves**.
   5. **Merge clients**.

Everything of the merged client moves to the kept one: bookings, sales, WhatsApp messages, intake forms, notes, packages, memberships, gift cards, conversations and waitlist entries. The merged profile is then deleted.

> **Important:** A merge cannot be undone. Accounts and receipts stay exactly as they are. Only roles with **Merge duplicate clients** can merge.

## Erase a client's personal data

If a client asks you to delete their data, you can erase it. Only the **Owner** can do this.

1. Open the client's profile and scroll down to **Erase personal data**.
2. Press **Erase client**.
3. If you need a copy, press **Download export** first.
4. Tick **I understand this permanently erases this client's personal data**.
5. Press **Erase permanently**. You see "Client data erased".

   ![Erasing personal data cannot be undone.](../../../shots/spa/c13-s08-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. What is removed and what is kept.
   2. **Download export**.
   3. The confirmation tick box.
   4. **Erase permanently**.

What is removed: the name, phone, email, birthday, nationality, tags, preferences, notes, treatment notes, intake forms, signatures and signed PDFs, WhatsApp messages and conversations, waitlist entries and booking notes.

What is kept for accounting: visits, sales, payments, packages and gift cards. They are shown as "Erased client".

> **Important:** This cannot be undone.
