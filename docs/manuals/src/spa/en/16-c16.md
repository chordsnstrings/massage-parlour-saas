# 16. WhatsApp messages (click to send)

## What this is for

Confirmations, reminders, thank-yous and offers are written for you and wait in a queue. You open each one in WhatsApp and press send yourself.

> **Important:** The CRM never sends WhatsApp messages, SMS or emails to clients on its own. A person presses send, every time, from your own WhatsApp.

## The WhatsApp queue

1. Open **Inbox & follow-ups** → **WhatsApp**.
2. The numbers at the top show **Due now**, **Scheduled** and **Sent today**.
3. In the **WhatsApp queue**, choose a tab:
   - **Due now**: send these now;
   - **Scheduled**: waiting until they are due;
   - **Sent**: what was sent in the last seven days.
4. Under **Send with**, choose how WhatsApp opens on this device: **WhatsApp Web**, **Desktop app** or **Phone**.

Each card has a label for its kind of message: **Confirmation**, **Reminder**, **Reminder (2 h)**, **Thank you**, **Review request**, **Rebook nudge**, **Birthday**, **Win-back**, **Free slot offer**, **Waitlist slot**, **Custom** or **Membership renewal**.

> **Tip:** On the computer at reception, choose **WhatsApp Web** or **Desktop app**. On a phone, choose **Phone**.

When everything is sent, the queue says "All caught up".

## Send a message

1. On a card, press **Open in WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with the text ready to send.
2. Press send in WhatsApp.
3. Come back to the CRM and press **Mark sent**. You see "Marked as sent".
4. If a message should not go, press **Skip**.

   ![Open, send, mark sent.](../../../shots/spa/c16-s02-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The message text. Press **Show full message** for long messages.
   2. **Open in WhatsApp**.
   3. **Mark sent**.
   4. **Skip**.

On a computer you can use the keyboard:

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **J** or **↓** | Next message |
| **K** or **↑** | Previous message |
| **Enter** | **Open in WhatsApp** |
| **S** | **Mark sent** |
| **X** | **Skip** |

> **Tip:** If WhatsApp did not open properly, press **Open again**.

> **Important:** Only press **Mark sent** after you really pressed send in WhatsApp. The CRM cannot see your WhatsApp. If you see "This message was already handled.", someone else on your team has already sent or skipped it.

## Share the work between receptionists

1. Above the cards, choose **All**, **Mine** or **Unassigned**.
2. Tick the messages you want to share, or tick **Select all**.
3. In the bar that appears, choose a team member and press **Assign**. Press **Clear selection** to cancel.

   ![Assign messages to a team member.](../../../shots/spa/c16-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **All**, **Mine** and **Unassigned**.
   2. A ticked message.
   3. The assign bar: the team member and **Assign**.

You can also assign one message with the list at the bottom right of its card.

4. To share due messages automatically, switch on **Assign WhatsApp messages** in **Automations** (chapter 27). Due messages then go round-robin to the receptionists on shift. The WhatsApp page says "Auto-assign is on: due messages go round-robin to receptionists on shift."

> **Tip:** The second, dark-green number next to **Inbox & follow-ups** in the left menu shows the WhatsApp messages assigned to you.

## Message templates

The templates are the words your team sends, in English and Arabic. Each client gets the language set on their profile.

1. On the WhatsApp page, press **Templates**.
2. On the left, choose a kind of message, for example **Confirmation**. Under its name you see when it is sent, for example "Sent as soon as a booking is confirmed."
3. Edit the **English** and the **Arabic** text.
4. Under **Insert a variable**, press a chip to add it to the English message where your cursor is: **{first_name}**, **{service}**, **{day}**, **{time}**, **{spa}**, **{ref}** or **{link}**. The CRM fills them in for each client.
5. Check the **Preview** and the **Preview (Arabic)**.
6. Press **Save template**. Press **Reset to default** to go back to the original text.

   ![Edit what your team sends.](../../../shots/spa/c16-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The kinds of message.
   2. The **English** and **Arabic** text.
   3. **Insert a variable**.
   4. The template with its status (here **Customised**) and the preview.
   5. **Reset to default** and **Save template**.

> **Tip:** A template can have up to 1,000 characters. If you see "Unknown variable — use the chips above", a variable is misspelled. Use the chips instead of typing it.

Some examples of when messages are prepared:

- **Reminder**: the day before the appointment.
- **Reminder (2 h)**: two hours before the appointment.

## Send responsibly

1. Only message clients who have booked or visited.
2. Never use auto-senders, bulk tools or WhatsApp Web bots. WhatsApp bans numbers that do.
3. Keep a human pace. The **Send responsibly** box shows how many messages were sent today.

> **Important:** A person presses send, every time. This keeps your WhatsApp number safe and your clients happy.
