# 1. Welcome to your spa CRM

## What this is for

Your spa CRM is one place to run your spa: the calendar, clients, the till, staff, stock, accounts and your website's menu. This chapter explains the few rules that everything else follows, the two plans, and the web addresses you will use.

## What the CRM does, and the four golden rules

1. Open **app.spamanagement.co** and sign in (chapter 2).
2. Your spa opens on the **Dashboard** (see "Your Dashboard" below). Everything else is in the menu on the left.
3. Read the four rules below once. Every screen follows them.

### The four golden rules

1. **Payments are recorded, never charged.** The CRM does not take money from cards. You take the money with cash, your own card terminal or a bank transfer, and then record it. The **Sales** page says: "Record payments taken at the till — cash, your own card terminal or bank transfer."
2. **Customer messages are WhatsApp click-to-send only.** The CRM writes each message for you. A person opens it in WhatsApp and presses send, every time. The CRM never sends SMS and never emails your guests.
3. **The spamanagement.co studio designs and publishes your website.** You keep its menu, prices, team and contact details up to date in your dashboard. For any other change, use **Request a change** on the **Website** page (chapter 24).
4. **There is one contact address: ask@spamanagement.co.** Write to it for help, upgrades and questions about your bill.

> **Tip:** The CRM screens are in English and Thai. Everything your guests see (your website, the booking page, intake forms and WhatsApp messages) is in English and Arabic.

> **Important:** All times are UAE time (Dubai). All amounts are in AED. Prices include 5% VAT.

## Your Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first page you see after you sign in. It shows how your spa is doing today, or over the last 7 or 30 days.

1. Press **Dashboard** in the left menu.
2. To see one branch, press its name next to **All branches**. Press **All branches** to see the whole spa again. This switch shows only when your spa has more than one branch.
3. Choose the period: **Today**, **7 days** or **30 days**. Every card on the page follows your choice.
4. Press **Website** to open your live website in a new tab.
5. Press **View details** on a card to open the page behind it.

## Standard and Premium: what each plan includes

1. Check your plan at the foot of the left menu (the plan card) or on **Billing**.
2. If you open a Premium page on the Standard plan, you see "*{feature}* is part of Premium".
3. Press **Compare plans** to see the full price table on spamanagement.co. Press **Your subscription** to open **Billing**.
4. To upgrade, ask your account manager at **ask@spamanagement.co**. Premium switches on straight away. Nothing else changes.

### The two plans

| | **Standard** | **Premium** |
|---|---|---|
| Setup fee (one time) | AED 9,000 | AED 14,000 |
| Monthly price | AED 2,000 / month | AED 3,000 / month |
| Branches | One branch | More than one branch |
| AI & Instagram automation | No | Yes |
| Marketing tools | No | Yes |

The plan prices above exclude VAT. VAT is added only if it applies. Spas on the old plan of AED 24,000 a year keep that plan, with every feature, until it renews.

**Every plan includes:**

- Calendar, rooms and resources.
- The online booking page and the booking widget.
- Point of sale and tax invoices.
- Packages, gift cards and memberships.
- Clients, intake forms and visit history.
- Staff, the time clock, commission and payroll / WPS.
- Inventory, purchases and the warehouse.
- WhatsApp click-to-send confirmations and reminders.
- A website built by our studio, and your own domain.
- Reports, accounts and VAT.
- The dashboard in English and Thai.
- Two-step sign-in and an installable app.
- Website enquiries and website analytics.

**Premium adds:**

- **AI & Instagram automation:** the AI receptionist, the Instagram inbox with AI replies and booking from chat, AI insights, receipt scanning for expenses and **Ask AI**.
- **Marketing tools:** campaigns (win-back, birthday and quiet-slot offers), Google Business and Instagram posting (**Social posts**), review requests and AI review replies (**Reviews**), and **QR posters**.
- **More branches:** more than one active branch, each with its own hours, rooms, staff and reports.

> **Tip:** The spamanagement.co team can also switch Premium features on for your spa. When they do, the plan card says "Premium features are switched on for your spa".

## The web addresses you will use

1. **Your dashboard (this CRM):** **app.spamanagement.co**. Bookmark it, or install it as an app (chapter 4).
2. **Your website:** your free address **yourspa.spamanagement.co**, or your own domain once it is connected (chapter 24).
3. **Your online booking page for clients:** your website address followed by **/book**, for example yourspa.spamanagement.co/book.
4. On the **Website** page, press **Copy link** to copy your live website address. Press **View live site** to open it.

   ![The Website page has your live website link.](../../../shots/spa/c01-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Copy link**.
   2. **View live site**.
   3. **Your website**: a preview of your site. **Live** means it is published.
   4. **Services & prices**: the list of services that your website shows.
   5. **Request a change**: ask our studio for any other change (chapter 24).

> **Tip:** Changes to your services and prices, your team (with **Show on website**), opening hours and contact details show on your live website straight away (on an HTML design, once our studio has connected it to your dashboard). No publishing is needed.

> **Tip:** Once your own domain is connected and set as primary, visitors who open your free address are sent on to your own domain automatically.

> **Important:** Your web address (the "yourspa" part) is chosen when you apply. To change it later, write to ask@spamanagement.co. The platform team makes the change.
