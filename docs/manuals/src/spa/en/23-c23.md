# 23. Reports and website analytics

## What this is for

See how the spa really performs: how many clients rebook, how much each therapist hour earns, how full your rooms are, how many clients come back, and how much prepaid value you still owe. Website analytics show how visitors find and use your website.

## Reports

1. Open **Finance** → **Reports**.
2. Choose the **Branch**: **All branches** or one branch. There is no therapist filter: the tables below list each therapist by name.
3. Choose the **Period**: **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days**, **This month** or **Last month**. Under the title you see the dates and when your business day ends, for example "business days end at 02:00 (Dubai time)".
4. The four tiles give the headline numbers: **Rebooking rate**, **RevPATH**, **Room utilisation** and **Prepaid liability**.
5. Scroll down for the details (see the list below).
6. Press **Export to Excel** to download every report in one workbook.

What each report means:

- **Rebooking rate**: completed visits whose client already has another booking dated within the window you choose. Cancelled bookings don't count. You see, for example, "1,166 of 1,262 visits rebooked", per therapist.
- **Revenue per available treatment hour** (RevPATH): net treatment revenue (without VAT, after refunds, including package and membership sessions) divided by the therapists' available hours. Hours come from shifts, or from the time clock for anyone without shifts. The column **Hours from** says which.
- **Room utilisation**: booked treatment minutes divided by opening hours times rooms. Cancelled and no-show bookings are left out.
- **Client retention**: each row is the clients whose first visit was in that month. The columns **+1 mo** to **+6 mo** show the share who came back 1 to 6 months later.
- **Outstanding prepaid liability**: the unused value of gift cards, packages and memberships for the whole spa, as of a date you choose (**As of**, then **Show**). It is checked against your accounts. "Matches the ledger (accounts 2100 and 2110)." means everything agrees.

> **Tip:** Packages, memberships and gift cards can be used at any branch, so the prepaid liability is always for the whole spa.

> **Tip:** RevPATH needs shifts or clocked hours. Without them you see "No shifts or clocked hours in this period, so RevPATH can't be worked out." Plan shifts (chapter 8) or use the time clock (chapter 18).

## Website analytics

1. Open **Growth** → **Marketing** → **Analytics**.
2. Choose the date range at the top right: **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**.
3. The tiles show **Visitors**, **Page views**, **Online bookings** (with the share of visitors who booked) and **WhatsApp taps**.
4. **Visitors per day** shows unique visitors per day, in Dubai time.
5. The **Booking funnel** shows how many visitors moved to each step: **Visited the site**, **Started a booking**, **Completed a booking** and **Tapped WhatsApp**.
6. **Where visitors come from** lists the sources, for example **Instagram**, **Google search**, **Direct**, **Google Business Profile**, **WhatsApp**, **Facebook** or **Other websites**, with how many of them booked. Below it, **Devices** shows mobile, desktop and tablet.
7. **Top pages** lists the pages of your website with **Views**, **Visitors**, **Clicks** and **Bookings**.
8. **Sections** shows which parts of each page people actually see and tap.

> **Important:** Analytics are cookieless and private. No personal data is stored. Visitors are counted with a daily rotating code, so nobody can be followed from day to day.

> **Tip:** **Sections** helps you see what works. If a section that gets many taps sits low on a page, ask our studio to move it higher with **Request a change** on the **Website** page (chapter 24).

> **Tip:** Visits through QR posters show as "qr", and visits through the booking widget as "widget" (chapters 12 and 25).

Reports and website analytics are part of every plan.
