# 27. Automations

## What this is for

Switch the background helpers on or off: reminders, thank-yous, birthday and win-back drafts, renewals, digests and alerts. Automations only queue messages. Your team still sends each WhatsApp with one tap.

## Switch automations on or off

1. Open **System** → **Automations**.
2. Under **Runs on its own**, each automation shows what it does and when it runs, for example "On every booking", "Hourly" or "Daily 09:30". The card's top right shows how many are active.
3. Use the switch next to an automation to turn it on or off. You see, for example, "Birthday messages switched on".
4. **Last 24 hours** shows what ran for your spa, with the status **Done**, **Nothing to do** or **Failed**.

The automations:

| Automation | What it does | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Booking confirmations & reminders** | Queues a WhatsApp confirmation when a booking is confirmed, plus reminders the day before and 2 hours before. | On every booking |
| **Thank-you messages** | Queues a thank-you message after checkout. | After checkout |
| **Review requests** | Queues a WhatsApp review request a few hours after checkout. Needs your review link; off until you switch it on. | Hourly |
| **Birthday messages** | Queues a birthday greeting with your booking link on the client's birthday. Off until you switch it on. | Hourly |
| **Win-back messages** | Queues a "we miss you" note for clients who have not visited for a while. Off until you switch it on. | Hourly |
| **Quiet-slot offers** | Queues offers for quiet hours to regular clients (the AI studio slot filler). | 10:30 · 15:30 |
| **Package expiry** | Closes packages past their validity and books the unused value. | Nightly |
| **Membership renewals** | Marks memberships ending within 7 days as due, queues a WhatsApp renewal reminder and closes ended periods. | Nightly |
| **Instagram posts** | Publishes approved posts at their scheduled time. | Every 5 min |
| **Google reviews sync** | Pulls new Google reviews and drafts replies. | Every 2 h |
| **Weekly insights** | AI summary of last week with two things to try. | Mondays 08:00 |
| **Morning digest** | Notifies managers of today's bookings and requests still waiting. | Daily 09:30 |
| **Document expiry alerts** | Notifies managers 60, 30 and 7 days before staff documents expire. | Daily 09:00 |
| **Assign WhatsApp messages** | Shares due WhatsApp messages round-robin between receptionists on shift now. Off: everyone picks from the queue. | Every minute |
| **Nightly backups** | Encrypted database backup. **Always on**. | Nightly |
| **Domains & SSL** | Checks custom domains and certificates. **Always on**. | Every 10 min |

> **Important:** Review requests, birthday messages, win-back messages, quiet-slot offers, Instagram posts, Google reviews sync and weekly insights are part of Premium. On the Standard plan they show a **Premium** badge instead of a switch, and they never run.

> **Tip:** **Nightly backups** and **Domains & SSL** are run by the platform for every spa. You can't switch them off.

> **Tip:** **Last 24 hours** lists only runs that touched your spa. Switched-off automations are skipped.

## Timing of client message drafts

1. In the card **Client message drafts**, press **Save timing** (the pencil at the top right) to open the settings.
2. Set **Quiet hours from** and **Quiet hours until**, for example 22:00 and 09:00 (Dubai time). Drafts never fall due in these hours; they wait until the quiet hours end.
3. Paste your Google "write a review" link as the **Review link**. Review requests are only queued once it is set.
4. Set **Review request after checkout (hours)**: from 1 to 72.
5. Set **Win-back after (days without a visit)**: from 21 to 365.
6. Press **Save timing**. You see "Draft timing saved".
7. To change the wording of these messages, press **Edit texts**. It opens **WhatsApp** → **Templates** (chapter 16).

> **Important:** Built-in limits protect your clients: one review request per client in 90 days, one birthday message a year, one win-back per absence (at most 25 a day), no client gets two marketing messages within 7 days, and clients who opted out never get drafts.
