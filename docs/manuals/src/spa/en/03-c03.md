# 3. Finding your way around

## What this is for

Some parts of the screen never change: the left menu, the top bar, the section tabs, search, notifications and banners. Learn them once and every page feels familiar.

## The left menu

1. At the top you see your spa logo and name.
2. Below it is the profile menu, with your name and your role.
3. The menu is split into groups: **Workspace**, **People**, **Growth**, **Finance** and **System**.
4. A small number next to an item tells you how much is waiting:
   - next to **Calendar**: the bookings today;
   - next to **Bookings**: the bookings today that are waiting for confirmation;
   - next to **Inbox & follow-ups**: the messages, chats and enquiries to handle. A second, dark-green number shows the WhatsApp messages assigned to you.
5. At the bottom you find the plan card and the **SM Spa Management** badge. The badge opens spamanagement.co.

> **Tip:** You only see the menu items your role allows. As the owner you see everything. A receptionist, for example, sees fewer items (chapter 9).

## The top bar and section tabs

1. The top bar shows the name of the page and the group it belongs to.
2. Some menu items hold several pages. These pages appear as tabs under the top bar. For example, **Services & menu** has the tabs **Services & rooms**, **Packages & gifts**, **Inventory**, **Purchases** and **Warehouse**.
3. On the right of the top bar you find **Ask AI** (Premium), the notifications bell, the search box and the **EN** / **ไทย** language switch.

## Switch the language (English / Thai)

1. In the top bar, press **EN** for English or **ไทย** for Thai.
2. The whole dashboard changes language at once, including buttons and messages.
3. Names you typed yourself never change. This includes the names of clients, staff, services, rooms and products.
4. Your choice is saved to your login, so it is the same on every phone and computer you sign in on.

   ![The same calendar in Thai.](../../../shots/spa/c03-s03-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The language switch, with **ไทย** selected.
   2. The menu and the buttons in Thai.
   3. A name typed in the CRM (here the therapist **Nok**). It stays as typed.

> **Tip:** Each team member chooses their own language. A Thai therapist can use the dashboard in Thai while you use it in English.

> **Important:** The language switch only changes the dashboard. Your website, booking page, intake forms and WhatsApp messages stay in English and Arabic.

## Search anything

1. Press the search box in the top bar, or press **Ctrl K** (**⌘ K** on a Mac).
2. Type at least 2 letters: a name, a phone number, a booking reference or a receipt number.
3. The results are grouped into **Clients**, **Bookings**, **Receipts**, **Staff** and **Services**.
4. Use the ↑ and ↓ keys to move, **Enter** to open a result and **Esc** to close.

   ![Search finds clients, bookings, receipts, staff and services.](../../../shots/spa/c03-s04-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The search box (here "fat").
   2. A result group (here **Clients**).
   3. The keyboard hints.

> **Tip:** Press **Show more** under a group to see all matches in that group.

> **Important:** Searching by phone number only works for roles that may see phone numbers.

## Notifications

1. The bell in the top bar shows how many notifications are unread.
2. Press the bell to see the latest ones.
3. Press a notification to open the right page. Press **Mark all as read** at the top of the list to clear the unread ones.
4. Press **See all notifications** for the full list.
5. On the full list, choose **All** or **Unread**. **Mark all as read** is there too.

You get a notification, for example, for:

- a new online booking, or a booking waiting for confirmation;
- a new website enquiry;
- low stock at a branch or the warehouse;
- a document that needs renewing;
- AI drafts waiting for approval, and AI budget warnings (Premium);
- an overdue invoice, a payment reminder, read-only mode and "Full access restored";
- your weekly insights, and the morning summary "Today: *n* bookings".

> **Tip:** To get notifications on your phone, switch on **Notifications on this device** under **Account & security** (chapter 5).

## Banners at the top of the page

1. News from spamanagement.co appears as a banner. Press **×** (**Dismiss**) when you have read it.
2. A red bar means a platform invoice is overdue. Press **Pay now** (chapter 29).
3. On Premium, AI budget banners warn you when 80% of the monthly AI budget is used, and again when AI pauses at 100%.

   ![Announcements and payment reminders appear at the top.](../../../shots/spa/c03-s06-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. An announcement (here "New: printable gift vouchers") with its close button.
   2. The red payment bar with **Pay now**.

What the red bar can say:

- "Please pay your invoice to avoid your account being paused."
- "Invoice overdue — the dashboard becomes read-only on *date* unless it is paid."
- "Read-only: an invoice is unpaid past the grace period. Your website and online booking keep working; changes are blocked until it is paid."

If our team has paused your account for late payment, the bar says: "Your account is paused for late payment — the dashboard is read-only until your invoice is paid. Your website and online booking keep working." Our team lifts the pause after your payment (chapter 29).

> **Important:** In read-only mode you can still look at everything, and clients can still book online. You cannot make changes until the invoice is paid.

What the AI banners can say (Premium):

- "AI budget *percent* used this month — AI pauses when it is reached. Contact us to raise it."
- "AI paused: monthly AI budget reached — contact us. AI restarts on the 1st."
- "AI is switched off for your spa — contact us."
