# 17. Website enquiries

## What this is for

Guests can send you a message, or a booking request, from your website. They land here. You answer each one on WhatsApp. Nothing is emailed to the guest.

## Answer website enquiries

1. Open **Inbox & follow-ups** → **Enquiries**. You also get the notification "New website enquiry" when a message arrives.
2. Choose which enquiries to see: **New**, **Replied**, **Closed** or **All**. The number after each shows how many there are.
3. To find one, type in **Search name, phone or message** and press the search button.
4. Each enquiry shows the guest's name and mobile, the message and when it was **Received**. Under the message you see the page it was sent from, for example "From the /contact page". If the guest wrote in Arabic, you also see "Wrote in Arabic".
5. Press **Reply on WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens a chat with the guest, with the start of a reply ready, for example "Hi Khadija, thank you for your message to Serenity Spa & Wellness." The start is in Arabic when the guest wrote in Arabic.
6. Finish your answer in WhatsApp and press send there.
7. Back in the CRM, the enquiry moves to **Replied**. If you answered another way, for example by phone, press **Mark replied** yourself.
8. When nothing more is needed, press **Close**. To bring a closed enquiry back, open **Closed** and press **Reopen**.

> **Important:** Website enquiries are answered only on WhatsApp, by a person. The CRM never sends emails or SMS to guests.

> **Tip:** The number next to **Inbox & follow-ups** in the left menu includes new enquiries. Check it a few times a day so no guest waits long.

> **Tip:** Roles that may not see client phone numbers see "Phone hidden" instead of the number, and no **Reply on WhatsApp** button (chapter 9).

## Booking requests from your website

Some website designs, for example one our studio made just for your spa, have their own booking form. What a guest sends there arrives here as a booking request. Nothing is booked automatically.

1. A booking request has a line above the guest's notes, for example "Booking request · Hot stone massage · 90 min · 2 guests · 16 Oct 2026 at 18:00 · with Nok". Only what the guest chose appears.
2. Check the time on the **Calendar** and add the booking there (chapter 10).
3. Press **Reply on WhatsApp**. The reply starts, for example, "Hi Khadija, thank you for your booking request at Serenity Spa & Wellness for Hot stone massage on 2026-10-16 at 18:00." It is in Arabic when the guest wrote in Arabic. The treatment is named only when it matched your menu. Confirm the time or offer another one, and send it in WhatsApp.
4. The request moves to **Replied**. Press **Close** when nothing more is needed.

> **Important:** A booking request is not a booking. It is not on your calendar, and no therapist or room is kept free until you add it.

> **Tip:** If a request can't be sent, for example after many requests in a short time, some designs offer the guest to send it on WhatsApp instead. Those requests arrive in your WhatsApp chats, not in **Enquiries**.

> **Tip:** Booking requests use the same notification, "New website enquiry", and count in the number next to **Inbox & follow-ups**.

Website enquiries are part of every plan.
