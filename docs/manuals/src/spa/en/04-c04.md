# 4. Install the CRM as an app

## What this is for

Open your spa in one tap, full screen, on your phone, tablet or computer. You do not need an app store. The app is named after your spa, for example **Serenity Management**, and uses your spa logo as its icon.

## Install on Android or a computer (Chrome, Edge)

1. Sign in and open your spa.
2. On the **Dashboard**, the tip **Install *app name*** may appear. Press **Install app**. (Press **Not now** to hide the tip.)

   ![The install tip on the Dashboard.](../../../shots/spa/c04-s01-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. The tip **Install Serenity Management**.
   2. **Install app**.
   3. **Not now**.

3. Or open the profile menu (your name at the top of the left menu) and press **Install app**.

   ![Install app is also in the profile menu.](../../../shots/spa/c04-s01-b.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. **Account & security**.
   2. **Switch spa**.
   3. **Install app**.
   4. **Sign out**.

4. Confirm in the browser window. The app appears on your home screen or desktop.

> **Tip:** The app name is the first word of your spa name followed by "Management". "Serenity Spa & Wellness" becomes **Serenity Management**. A leading "The" or "Al" is skipped, so "Al Noor Spa" becomes **Noor Management**.

> **Tip:** The icon is your spa logo. Without a logo, the icon shows your spa initials on a lime background. Add a logo in **Settings** → **Spa profile** (chapter 6).

**Install app** only appears when your browser can install the app. If you do not see it, the app may already be installed on this device.

## Install on iPhone or iPad (Safari)

1. Open **app.spamanagement.co** in Safari and sign in.
2. Press **Install app** or **Show me how**. The steps appear.
3. Tap the Share button in the browser toolbar.
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Tap **Add**. The app appears on your Home Screen.
6. Press **Got it** to close the steps.

   ![The iPhone install steps.](../../../shots/spa/c04-s02-a.png)

   *In the screenshot:*

   1. Step 1: tap the Share button.
   2. Step 2: tap **Add to Home Screen**.
   3. Step 3: tap **Add**.
   4. **Got it**.

> **Important:** On an iPhone, notifications only work from the installed app, not from Safari. Install the app first, then switch on notifications (chapter 5).

If the internet connection drops, the app shows "You're offline". Press **Try again** when you are back online.
